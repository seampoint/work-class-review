# Explicit disposition of disputed evidence

Status: `DRAFT_NOT_ADOPTED`, `APPARATUS_ONLY`. This document defines explicit recovery for disputed reservation exposure and work outcomes. `reservation.schema.json` and `lifecycle.schema.json` supply the closed carriers. The source selects the recovery roles, negative-prerequisite consequence and permitted exception profiles. Recovery changes current accounting and eligibility only through the transitions below. Historical candidates, completed routes and authority acts retain their original meaning.

## REC-001. Selection and initial state

A registry or work definition selects recovery through its optional `recovery` configuration. The configuration names the protected governance provider/source, controller role, policy-owner role, admitted policy families, exception profiles and `negative_prerequisite_behavior:STOP`. The provider/source identify organizational governance custody. They do not replace the operation's declared native-effect or no-effect provider. Configuration identifiers and family arrays are sorted and unique. A selected registry starts with `core.recovery:{policies:[],dispositions:[],contingent:[],allowances:[]}`. A selected work instance starts with `recovery_records:[]`. Missing selected state, a recovery event without selection, or nonempty recovery state without its selection is invalid. Existing definitions without recovery keep their original state shape and behavior.

The four registry recovery collections, `policies`, `dispositions`, `contingent` and `allowances`, use ascending canonical-byte order of their complete rows. Their identifiers and accounting relationships remain subject to the uniqueness rules below. Work recovery applications retain lifecycle event order for checkpoint reversal.

Registry `RECOVERY_POLICY` and `DISPOSE` events use the existing revisioned event envelope and null ordinary administration. Work-only `DISPOSE` and registry `APPLY_DISPOSITION` use the existing lifecycle event envelope, expected state digest/revision and accepted clock revision. All new acts, evidence, state and receipts are complete records under the selected candidate pin. An unchanged prior candidate state cannot be silently relabelled, migrated or interpreted as selecting recovery.

## REC-002. Targets, identities and replay

A reservation target is `{kind:RESERVATION,registry_digest,reservation}` and resolves to one existing reservation under that exact configuration. A work target is `{kind:WORK_OUTCOME,instance_id,occurrence_id,outcome_digest}` and resolves to one retained governed outcome of that occurrence. `recovery-target` hashes the complete target. The request in a recovery packet is the complete reservation native-request projection, including work class, instance, occurrence, step, interface, operation and exact fields. Every attempt's request digest is `digest("reservation-native-request",request)`.

`recovery-fact` hashes the complete native record after omitting only `id`, `evidence_id`, `evidence_ref`, `record_digest` and `observed_at`. All other fields remain. `recovery-generation` hashes exactly `{target,request,covered_evidence_digests,superseded_evidence_digests,attempts,conclusion,native_outcomes,rules_out_past_and_future_effects}`. Covered/superseded fact inventories are sorted unique; attempts sort by their unique identifier; `native_outcomes` is replaced by the sorted unique canonical native projections used by `recovery-fact`. Report identities and packet wrappers do not create a new generation. Relevant facts, attempts or authoritative supersession relationships do. The complete packet separately has `digest("recovery-evidence",packet)`.

A reservation incident is `digest("recovery-incident",{principal,registry_digest,reservation,occurrence,error_class:"DUPLICATE_SEND_UNCERTAINTY"})`, using the originating reservation's exact occurrence. A work incident hashes `{principal,target}` under the same domain. An accepted exception basis is `digest("recovery-acceptance-basis",{incident_digest,generation_digest,policy_digest})`. A disposition identifier is `digest("recovery-disposition",{incident_digest,generation_digest,result,policy_digest,controller,supersedes})`; no identifier contains its own digest. Exact event replay returns its retained result with current state and no repeated charge, allowance or permission. Changed bytes under one event identity are `REPLAY_CONFLICT`.

A fresh event identifier with an existing successful acceptance basis returns `DISPOSITION_REUSED` only when its coverage and policy remain current; it references the established disposition and changes no contingent exposure or allowance. A withdrawn, expired or superseded policy cannot authorize a fresh acceptance through reuse. Exact original-event replay still returns its historical receipt and current state. A positive disposition cannot contradict another effective positive disposition of the same generation. Except for permitted reuse, a new decision after a retained current disposition explicitly supersedes that current disposition against current state; absent or wrong predecessor is `REVISION_CONFLICT`. A later generation or policy version always needs that fresh revision-checked decision. An earlier `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED` can be explicitly superseded by a positive decision for the same generation after its failed condition is supplied. Splitting an incident or partially overlapping its attempt inventory cannot create smaller acceptance bases.

## REC-003. Independent human recovery authority

Evidence-based `ESTABLISH_EFFECT` and `ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT` require one current host-selected HUMAN recovery designation and an accepted act with attributed return. The designation binds its actor, principal, configured controller role, `recovery-target` scope, permitted operation, validity, current observation instant, governance provider/source and unrevoked status. Its act binds that exact basis, actor, principal, role, operation, occurrence instant and complete recovery subject. For registry events the subject is `digest("recovery-subject",event_without_payload_authorization_and_payload_controller)`; for work `DISPOSE` it omits the top-level controller. Event identity, expected revision/digest, clocks, requested result and complete evidence remain in the subject. The return supplies the exact canonical act bytes, `digest("recovery-act",act)`, same actor, configured provider/source and current return instant. Its authenticated tuple uses `RECOVERY_RETURN` and `digest("recovery-return",returned)`.

For work recovery, the principal is the instance's retained `deployment_organization_id`, established by its deployment authorization. An optional authority act or a caller-supplied principal cannot replace that selection.

For payment adjudication, the controller differs from the affected executor and human payment releaser selected by the original authority. For a work-only supplier-master prerequisite, it differs from that occurrence's executor and destination-confirmation actor; it needs no identity for an unselected future payment releaser. Compare exact authenticated actor identifiers. One ordinary registry-administrator designation or business credential supplies no recovery remit. Missing, revoked, expired, non-HUMAN, wrong-role, wrong-operation or non-ACCEPT recovery authority refuses `ADMINISTRATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. Binding mismatches refuse `DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH`; absent authenticated returns refuse `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`.

For disputed no-effect release, the same exact controller designation must expressly include `RELEASE_DISPUTED_EXPOSURE` in addition to `ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT`. Its acceptance is the complete organizational release act for this disposition, binding the target, evidence, attempts and released contributions. No separate ordinary RELEASE act is required. An adjudication-only controller cannot release exposure; an ordinary RELEASE act cannot dispose of a dispute. Ordinary RES-008 release remains unchanged.

## REC-004. Complete authoritative evidence

The recovery packet has record type `RECOVERY_ADJUDICATION`. Its independently authenticated provider/source match the original selected settlement/no-effect provider pair for reservation adjudication, or the target work step's completion provider pair. The host's `RECOVERY_EVIDENCE` tuple binds `digest("recovery-evidence",packet)` and that provider/source. The protected packet names the exact target/request, provider revision, full relevant fact inventory, all attempts, authoritative conclusion, accepted native conclusions and every superseded contradictory conclusion. `superseded_evidence_digests` is a subset of covered facts and names the provider's authoritative rejection or replacement relationship. Authentication establishes who supplied that relationship; the consumer checks its complete binding and coverage. An unsupported human preference, model assessment or caller-selected favorable record supplies no authoritative relationship.

Covered fact identities equal the full relevant retained target fact set, including packet native conclusions not already retained. Known attempts cannot be omitted. The provider's `complete_attempt_inventory:true` asserts a finite complete inventory across the invocation, connector and every writer. A registry without a dispatch ledger checks every retained known attempt and relies on the protected provider/host assertion for completeness beyond it. Each attempt binds its original request digest, executor, connector, finite positive maximum executions, known effect identities and final no-effect flag. Effect identities are unique within their actual execution and cannot be allocated to another attempt. Conflicting or missing grouping, request attribution, evidence coverage or attempt coverage refuses new clearance.

`DISPOSE` evaluates its supplied current complete histories even when the selected native fact was already retained. Native-fact replay continues to ignore new history under RES-WIRE-003; it cannot substitute for this transition. Current histories preserve their complete journal prefixes, provider bindings, freshness and projection rules. Ordinary unknown outcomes retain their single reservation without acquiring a dispute block. Recovery creates no permission merely because an unknown outcome exists.

## REC-005. Policy issuance, supersession and withdrawal

The separately configured HUMAN policy-owner designation expressly permits `ISSUE_POLICY` or `REVOKE_POLICY` for the exact `digest("recovery-policy-scope",policy.scope)`. Scope names the principal, registry, budget anchors, work classes, eligible executors/connectors, unit, exception profile and window. The complete accepted policy-owner act and returned canonical bytes bind the `RECOVERY_POLICY` event under REC-003's subject construction. A registry administrator, executor or caller cannot infer this remit. The same person may hold controller and policy-owner roles only through separate designations; target-specific controller independence still applies.

`ISSUE` includes a complete policy, its exact `recovery-policy` digest and family. Initial issuance has null supersedes. A subsequent version names the exact current policy digest, has a strictly later natural version and retains identical family scope. Both limits and validity are explicit; validity has a finite nonnull end strictly after its start, and expiry is exclusive under the interval rules. The family is admitted by configuration. Its first profile is `MONETARY_DUPLICATE_SEND`, nonnegative monetary SUM in USD with window `86400` seconds. A changed principal, registry, scope inventory, unit or window is `UNSUPPORTED_RELATION` and requires a separately designed migration. New identifiers or versions cannot reset family history. Each complete retained policy version has a unique policy identifier; the family supplies the stable identity across versions. Reusing a policy identifier for different content is `DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH`.

`REVOKE` includes the complete exact current policy and digest, with the accepted policy-owner act bound to that family/scope. It ends ongoing and future clearance under that policy while retaining its issuance, decisions, charges and allowances. The current policy record marks revocation; the immutable issuance/withdrawal events and receipts establish its history. Each successful policy transition returns `POLICY_RECORDED`. Policy issuance grants no particular work act and performs no business operation.

## REC-006. Established effect and exact accounting

`ESTABLISH_EFFECT` requires REC-003 authority and a complete packet establishing the exact attributable effects. Each established execution has a distinct native effect identity, occurrence instant, exact result and corresponding prescribed committed journal entries under every affected projection. The packet explicitly resolves every covered conclusive contradiction. The same native effect identity is charged once even when reported repeatedly. A genuinely second execution remains a second effect, including one with identical arguments.

The transition evaluates each original possible execution against the complete accepted attempt/effect inventory. An established execution replaces its own pending or contingent contribution with its actual committed contribution once. An affirmatively excluded execution removes only its own uncertainty. Uncovered or unresolved possible executions remain charged. Existing actual journal contributions are never also counted as their own pending uncertainty; they retain ordinary committed window membership. COUNT, SUM and CARDINALITY use their existing exact contribution/reducer meanings. Unresolved exceptions support only REC-009's narrower monetary SUM profile.

A successful effect disposition is retained independently of historical disputed effects and flags. Those historical records remain unchanged. Current blockers and pending substitution derive from the exact effective disposition and accepted history. A changed destination, amount, invoice, native identity or extra actual effect remains visible. It cannot be relabelled as the original authorized completion. Accounting retains all established charges even when changed facts cannot restore dependent work. A repaired journal can establish a formerly uncorroborated original effect without adding another native identity. The successful receipt is `DISPOSITION_RECORDED` with the disposition digest.

## REC-007. Established no effect and covered release

`ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT` requires the independently designated controller and its explicit disputed-exposure release remit. The complete authoritative packet must exclude both past and future execution of every covered outstanding attempt and supersede every contrary conclusive assertion. A cancellation request, empty query, timeout, permit expiry, stopped instance or withdrawn business grant is insufficient. An actual established contribution, uncovered attempt or unrelated uncertainty remains counted. The transition removes only the pending/contingent contributions whose possible executions are affirmatively closed. It retains the exact release evidence, controller act and before/after accounting through its event and receipt history.

No effect is an unsuccessful operation outcome. Local application follows the operation's declared failure behavior and cannot mark success. Source revision 2 selects STOP for the supplier payment. A new payment requires separately authorized fresh work with current authority and evidence that the old invocation cannot execute later. This profile performs no retry, refund or compensation. No financial no-effect disposition releases an exclusive equipment identifier; its existing source-selected retention continues independently.

## REC-008. Conservative contingent exposure

Every possible execution of a disputed invocation remains represented. The original reservation supplies the first unresolved execution once. Additional finite possible executions use current `contingent` rows carrying the originating reservation, exact attempt/index, original budget projection, key and typed value. Their identity is `digest("recovery-contingent",{registry_digest,reservation,attempt_id,execution_index,anchor,mapping,key})`. They are retained pending exposure under `INCLUDE_ALL_RESERVED`, independently of age and ordinary committed-history windows. They are neither additional reservations for the same occurrence nor fabricated committed journal entries. A retained disposition with a complete finite attempt inventory, including an explicit LEAVE_UNRESOLVED, replaces the reservation's current contingent rows with that complete inventory, and each row's `disposition_digest` names that disposition.

Before any exception clears a block, the complete bounded contingent inventory commits atomically with that decision. Equal arguments or a caller-supplied idempotency token provide no execution bound. An authenticated provider guarantee must cover the exact requests, identities and relevant writers to collapse attempts. Known actual executions occupy their exact slots and replace those possible charges once. New no-effect evidence closes only named slots. A changed request, additional unsupported attempt or unbounded multiplicity preserves the applicable block; the consumer never publishes smaller exposure by truncating an inventory.

When a structurally admitted finite incident fails its incident, cumulative, policy or ordinary-budget conditions, retain a `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED` disposition, the complete attempted source event and all bounded contingent charges. Return `WITHHELD` and the precise failed reasons. Its acceptance basis is null and it consumes no allowance. A failed cap does not erase evidence of additional possible executions. An admission failure grants nothing and leaves governed state unchanged; protected pending evidence continues to prevent reliance on earlier clearance under REC-016.

A known native EFFECT whose numerical accounting cannot yet be established remains retained actual evidence; it is not zero exposure. For each affected numerical budget, its observed request must project to exact typed contributions and each contribution must have its exact prescribed committed record in a usable current complete history before that actual accounting is assessed. Examine relevant unsuperseded native effects retained in native records and recovery packets under the existing authenticated coverage, supersession and retention rules, including packets retained by explicit or failed LEAVE_UNRESOLVED. An actual attributable to the originating invocation remains relevant when its observed request or key changed. Native supersession does not delete committed journal amounts or bypass existing journal-prefix and retraction rules. Merely listing a digest as covered neither establishes numerical accounting nor classifies pending evidence as irrelevant. Conflicting actual assertions remain unresolved until authoritative supersession resolves them. If any required actual contribution is unprojectable or uncorroborated, the affected numerical exposure is unassessed: do not derive or publish an ordinary aggregate result for that event. Empty budgets in this condition mean no assessed amount, never zero exposure.

Remaining possible executions retain REC-008's original-projection pending and contingent representation. An actual execution remains visible in its exact retained native evidence while its accounting is unassessed; those original-projection rows do not assert its changed actual amount. Once actual accounting is corroborated, count each actual contribution through its committed history once. The original reservation supplies the first remaining possible execution when its current state includes that pending contribution; otherwise retain a contingent row for every remaining possible execution. Omitting an otherwise pending original contribution requires either the existing authorized no-effect release or a current complete covering attempt inventory with at least one exactly corroborated actual execution and no remaining possible execution. An incomplete inventory or LEAVE containing only asserted no-effect closures does not independently authorize that omission. Count this exact pending representation in the REC-016 resource preflight. Independent operational disputes remain blocked; accounting substitution does not turn LEAVE_UNRESOLVED into permission.

## REC-009. Bounded unresolved exception

`ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED` applies only to an operational duplicate-send uncertainty with unchanged exact monetary request arguments, complete finite attempt inventory and authenticated upper bounds on executions per attempt. Every affected aggregate is nonnegative SUM in USD, with complete projectable grouping and current required history. Changed arguments, contradictory conclusive facts, known additional actual effects, foreign effects, unavailable required history, unknown grouping, nonmonetary reducers or unbounded executions are ineligible. Exact duplicate reports of one attempt create neither an additional possible execution nor allowance consumption.

The current policy names the exact registry, budgets, work class and every involved executor/connector. The authenticated caller is within its executor application scope. Selection, current validity, unrevoked state and all limits pass deterministically. The human policy owner's accepted preapproval supplies authority for that bounded application; no case-specific controller act is required. Each accepted incident measures its whole unresolved case exposure, including the original reservation and every additional possible execution. Two bounded 2 USD attempts measure 4 USD; two bounded 5 USD attempts measure 10 USD. The provisional source chooses 5 USD per acceptance and 10 USD over rolling 24 hours. Those amounts belong to its policy, not a universal contract limit.

Acceptance clears only the covered operational-noise block for otherwise eligible independent work. It never establishes the uncertain operation as successful, satisfies a factual prerequisite, releases its charges, authorizes another send, revives a consumed permit or waives an ordinary budget limit. A reservation partition with any other unresolved dispute remains blocked. An accepted uncertainty is retained as `ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED` with its exact nonnull acceptance basis and policy digest.

## REC-010. Allowance accounting and ordinary limits

Each successful acceptance consumes its full measured case amount once for every applicable budget and each distinct involved executor and connector. Allowance records retain `{acceptance_basis_digest,policy_family,budget,actor_kind,actor,amount,accepted_at}` in canonical byte order. Budget attribution uses its anchor as actor. Executor/connector counters do not multiply ordinary financial exposure. For one budget/actor/family, sum admissions in `(now-86400 seconds,now]` using the registry's accepted governance clock. All applicable limits and ordinary budget constraints pass in the same transition. No partial acceptance is possible.

For a finite projectable `ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED` request with usable supplied histories, derive one aggregate evaluation for every affected anchor in anchor order, even when a policy, eligibility, incident or allowance condition also fails. Its new operation is the originating reservation's exact authorized native projection and original occurrence key. Pending includes other ordinary reservations, existing contingent rows outside this incident, and the complete current incremental contingent inventory for this incident; exclude the originating ordinary reservation so its contribution appears once. For aggregate assessment, a contingent row projects to `{reservation:row.id,mapping:row.mapping,occurrence:row.id,key:row.key,contribution:row.value}`. Its retained `occurrence` still identifies the original source occurrence. This separate possible-execution identity prevents an aggregate pending/new-operation collision. Sort pending under AGG's canonical key, derive the ordinary history query using that original operation, supplied current history, event clock and candidate pin, and retain `{anchor,result}` in receipt budgets. A bound violation adds `BOUND_VIOLATED`; another nonsatisfied aggregate adds `HISTORY_INCOMPLETE`. Unusable histories add their existing history reasons and supply no fabricated evaluation. Nonprojectable or unbounded cases have no invented aggregate result. This assessment creates no native operation. The pending rows derived for each ordinary aggregate request remain subject to AGG's 1024-member collection bound. Preflight that derived inventory before evaluating or committing the disposition. More than 1024 rows is LIMIT_EXCEEDED with no partial state change. REC-016's 4096-row recovery inventory bound is independent and does not enlarge an aggregate request's collection bound.

A new relevant generation or newly authorized policy version requires a fresh acceptance of the complete current case and consumes its full amount again. Prior allowance entries remain until their windows expire; resolution does not refund them. This conservative rule prevents renamed requests, split inventories and new policy versions from restoring allowance. Exhausting a cumulative allowance prevents another acceptance without invalidating an otherwise current prior acceptance. Ordinary unresolved original/contingent exposure remains charged when the allowance or committed-history window rolls over. A 1,000 USD ordinary ceiling still forbids 1,001 USD regardless of allowance availability.

Missing, conflicting or unavailable family history withholds acceptance. The current policy's limits apply across retained family history. Supersession cannot change the namespace, scope or counting window to bypass a cumulative limit. Admission beyond supported bounds fails before partial records or counter changes.

The requirement to evaluate affected ordinary aggregates applies only when all required histories and affected actual accounting are assessable. If any affected required actual is unassessed, REC-008's exception makes the entire receipt carry empty budgets without ordinary aggregate evaluation, including for failed ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED. Continue all independently applicable evidence, eligibility and policy checks. An incident or cumulative cap is assessable only when every actual amount needed for its whole-incident amount is established; then count each actual contribution once plus the remaining possible original contributions. If that amount is unassessed, grant no allowance and report no fabricated cap pass or violation. Do not use an original-projection placeholder total as the changed actual amount. REC-016's unconditional recovery-inventory arithmetic preflight remains required. AGG's 1024-member bound still applies whenever an ordinary aggregate request is actually derived.

## REC-011. Continuing coverage and renewed conflict

A disposition clears only its exact relevant fact/attempt generation. New attributable relevant evidence outside that coverage invalidates clearance until admitted classification or a fresh superseding disposition covers it. An unchanged duplicate native report does not create a generation. Required current histories and independent unresolved blocks retain their own force. A locally cached disposition cannot establish current registry selection, current evidence completeness or current exception policy validity.

At every affected proposal and dispatch boundary, establish the current selected registry/work evidence, target-bound pending inventory, coverage and applicable policy validity. Expiry, supersession or revocation of an exception policy ends its ongoing clearance for future permission. Existing results, effects, charges and past allowances remain. New effects after a no-effect disposition restore conservative exposure and relevant blocks; the earlier release remains historical. A stopped or complete instance still admits attributable reconciliation without reopening scheduling.

## REC-012. Work-only adjudication

A work `DISPOSE` targets an exact governed outcome and occurrence in its own instance. It uses current lifecycle clock selection, the complete source event and separate `recovery_host` evidence. It supports established effect, established no effect and leave unresolved. An unresolved monetary exception is registry-owned and cannot be fabricated as a work-only decision. A nonfinancial supplier-master prerequisite creates no zero-value reservation or external case store.

For a previously completed prerequisite, established effect must preserve the original exact factual basis. For an unproposed descendant, compare the establishing ancestor's retained native fact under the definition's prior-effect bindings. For an already permitted descendant, compare the exact prior-effect bases retained by that permit. A new value cannot rewrite those bases. If authoritative evidence establishes the original required prerequisite false or materially different, the selected STOP consequence stops an ACTIVE affected instance, retains historical completions/routes and all financial uncertainty, and creates no successor. An already STOPPED or COMPLETE instance retains its terminal status.

## REC-013. Application and safe restoration

`APPLY_DISPOSITION` selects a complete current registry state, its digest and protected selection evidence, and names an effective retained reservation disposition. It checks the registry against its event/receipt reconstruction before applying that disposition to the local instance. Application changes only local evidence-derived eligibility and lifecycle consequences; registry accounting is already committed. Each affected instance applies against its own expected revision, original factual bases and current evidence. Independent registry work need not wait for an unrelated instance to apply. There is no global transaction across every instance or external provider.

An active descendant leaves `BLOCKED_DISPUTE` only when every blocking ancestor is established on its original factual basis. Preserve unrelated causes. A dispatch-disqualified attempt that may have affected the connector is a separate unresolved cause when it blocks a still-active source or the active descendants of a completed source, regardless of gate form. A `DISPUTED` row without a dispatch-disqualifying reason is likewise a separate cause while its source is blocked, either because the step is gated or because the stable attempt is not a LIFE-010 final safe attempt. A later `NOT_SENT`-to-`ACCEPTED` acknowledgement update for an attempt not bound to the completed source's conclusive native outcome is a separate cause when it blocks that source's active descendants under LIFE-013. The disqualifying reasons are exactly `NATIVE_ARGUMENT_MISMATCH`, `PERMIT_EXPIRED`, `PERMIT_SUPERSEDED` and `LATE_OR_PROHIBITED_DISPATCH`; `REJECTED` does not establish absence of effect. Clearing an unrelated ancestor or reservation dispute cannot demote the blocked source or restore those descendants. A lookup failure, unknown native report, unrelated disposition or `ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED` cannot clear the dispatch cause, even when an exception attempt inventory includes the attempt. A covering effective disposition must account for every retained physical attempt and establish that no remaining possible execution can change the operation's original factual basis. Accounting for an extra effect does not grant retroactive send authority or a normal success route. The current recovery carriers require a retained outcome or reservation target. A dispatch-only block with neither target has no portable clearance in this candidate and remains blocked. If later attributable evidence creates a target, only an effective disposition expressly covering the disputed attempt and preserving the original basis may address the cause under the existing recovery rules; it does not reopen a stopped instance. A STOPPED or COMPLETE instance remains terminal. An unproposed descendant returns to `ACTIVE`, regains proposal eligibility and then needs fresh current authority, separation, budget, observations and clock checks. An unchanged permitted descendant whose attempt definitely was not sent returns to `PERMITTED` and cannot dispatch its old permit; the retained recovery application supplies that continuing restriction. It requires fresh current renewal under the existing expiry/renewal rules, including retained expiry before renewal. Attempting to dispatch that old restored permit refuses `PREREQUISITE_MISSING`. An already sent, possibly sent, consumed, expired or superseded dispatch authorization never revives. Changed arguments require new work. A conclusive local outcome retained while blocked can be applied once only after its original dependencies resolve and no terminal route or deadline prevents it.

Historical outcomes keep their disputed flags. Effective factual selection derives from exact current admissible disposition coverage, and original retained records supply the value bindings. A receipt references the applied disposition digest. Missing current selection, coverage or pending-evidence classification withholds affected permission. Application cannot infer current authority from a historical controller act or readiness result.

## REC-014. Obligations, deadlines and terminal history

Definitions selecting [TIMING.md](TIMING.md) retain event_duties and deferred_deadlines in both recovery projections, exactly when selected. Checkpoint reversal restores those inventories. Initial event anchors remain immutable deployment evidence outside the mutable projection. Recovery preserves the original trigger, due and expiry basis and consumes stored expiry before restored eligibility.

A pre-join completion frontier may restore its original discharge basis only after every disputed member resolves on that original basis. Recompute nested outward waiting obligations, preserving unresolved children and independent causes. Fire an eligible all-obligations join once in the same local transition. Already JOINED obligations and completed occurrence routes remain unchanged; recovery cannot rewind a route or actuation counter.

A deadline that expired while work was blocked retains its due instant and follows its already declared expiry consequence when safely reconsidered. Recovery grants no extension, ignores no committed expiry and cannot make an earlier observation retroactively win over it. Fresh activation still requires its declared available clock and limits. If it cannot satisfy them, retain evidence and apply the existing declared stop behavior. STOPPED and COMPLETE instances retain evidence/accounting application without reactivation, new routing or joining. General cancellation and compensation remain outside this profile.

## REC-015. Retention and state reconstruction

Registry dispositions retain their exact target, incident/generation/basis identities, result, complete evidence, policy/controller, supersession, accepted instant and full source event. Registry event records already retain host evidence and receipts. Policies retain exact issuance/withdrawal history; allowance and disposition histories are append-only. Current contingent inventory is reconstructed from those events and accepted attempts, including removed slots whose possibilities were authoritatively closed. A current inventory cannot omit a still-possible charge.

Each lifecycle recovery application retains its complete source event, recovery host evidence, exact disposition, and exact before/after projection of status, active/completed occurrences, obligations, deadlines, counters and fan-out passes. Append-only original outcomes, proposals, permits, dispatches and receipts remain. Restart validates receipt/event bindings, candidate identity, authority/evidence preimages, registry selection and supersession, reverses the retained projection to reconstruct earlier checkpoints, and recomputes the declared consequence. A digest with no complete preimage cannot establish a release or cleared block. Reconstruction must reject altered authority, omitted pending exposure, fabricated allowance history, incompatible dispositions or lifecycle clearance without an effective disposition.

When `stepInput.recovery_registries` supplies a nonempty selection inventory, the application also retains that exact inventory as `registry_selections`. This field is required for such an application and omitted when the input inventory is empty or absent. It contains at most one complete current registry selection. Reconstruction supplies these retained selections when re-evaluating a recovery consequence, including a previously retained budget-backed local completion whose prerequisite resolves during work-only adjudication. An omitted or altered selection cannot establish that completion or its resulting route.

A lifecycle recovery consequence that stops an ACTIVE instance returns the existing `STOPPED` disposition and the recovery disposition digest. Disproving a previously completed required prerequisite adds `RECOVERY_BASIS_CHANGED`; applying no effect to an unresolved operation retains its ordinary declared failure reason. Other successful work adjudications return `DISPOSITION_RECORDED`; successful registry applications return `DISPOSITION_APPLIED`. A pure restoration has empty reason and detail inventories. A recovery-induced declared expiry, join or route retains its ordinary required reason/detail records under REC-014. An established local completion derives its exact native basis from the retained recovery packet and application record; it need not fabricate an earlier EFFECT_OBSERVED event. Its unchanged original result bindings, routing and completion invariants still apply.

Every lifecycle recovery receipt has null `permit_digest` and `authority_result_digest`. Recovery issues no permit and performs no new business-authority evaluation; it cannot select one arbitrary original permit when several retained descendants complete. The recovery disposition digest identifies the retained application, and original permits and factual bases remain in state.

When complete authoritative evidence establishes multiple distinct actual executions for an unresolved local operation and no unique original established native basis identifies its completion, retain the complete factual packet and unchanged local projection with empty reasons and details; create no completion, route or actuation. Work-only `DISPOSE` returns `DISPOSITION_RECORDED`; `APPLY_DISPOSITION` returns `DISPOSITION_APPLIED`.

For local completion from a reservation disposition, derive the lifecycle native record from the selected packet native conclusion. Its `evidence_id` is native `id`; `native_operation_id` is native `effect_id`, or native `id` for no effect; provider/source and evidence reference come from that native conclusion. Its record type is the original step's declared effect or no-effect record type, and its record digest is the recovery packet's `record_digest`. Its attributed request is native `request`, with the existing `native-request` digest. Status is `EFFECT_ESTABLISHED` or `NO_EFFECT_ESTABLISHED` according to native `outcome`. Copy exact actual fields, collections, `occurred_at` into `event_time`, and the past-and-future exclusion flag; `observed_at` is the disposition's accepted instant. The completed occurrence binds this complete derived record through the existing `native-evidence` digest. It never substitutes a superseded historical outcome merely because its status matches. A work-only packet already carries a complete lifecycle native conclusion and needs no such conversion.

## REC-016. Commitment, pending evidence and bounds

One registry disposition atomically commits its accounting, contingent inventory, allowance entries, retained decision and event receipt. A work-only disposition atomically commits its evidence and local lifecycle projection. Applying a registry disposition commits only the affected instance's local transition. The host serializes current selected state and revision checks with commitment before future dispatch; a failed write publishes none of that intended transition. External provider mutation fences and durable persistence remain explicit host capabilities, separate from schema admission or hashing.

Permit-issuance checks for permits backed by numerical budgets or exclusive keys are specified in LIFE-008 and LIFE-009A. Fresh dispatch-authorization and native-dispatch checks under those permits are specified in LIFE-009A. They require equality with the whole registry revision retained in the permit, so any later revision, including an unrelated revision, makes that reference stale. Pending-evidence custody, serialization with durable commitment, retention of checked-state evidence and fail-closed behavior are specified there as well. These are host obligations and add no fields to the closed AUTHORIZE_DISPATCH event; a consumer STEP does not establish them or grant dispatch permission. Provider mutation fences and safe retry after uncertain submission remain separate host obligations.

The host protects incoming attributable relevant evidence before application, including when evaluation or state commitment fails. Its target-bound pending inventory participates in affected proposal/dispatch permission until admission or a retained classification establishes irrelevance. A failed state write cannot expose earlier clearance while the new relevant fact waits outside governed state. Malformed or unattributable input does not by itself block unrelated work. At such a boundary, missing pending-evidence classification refuses `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE` or records `RECOVERY_PENDING_EVIDENCE` where a retained policy withholding applies. Protected custody, complete writer coverage and current selection are host assertions requiring separate deployment evidence.

A packet supports at most 4096 attempts, native outcomes, covered facts and superseded facts; an attempt supports at most 4096 effect identities. Current contingent inventory has at most 4096 rows. One scope supports at most 64 budgets, work classes, executors, connectors, policy families or selected designations. Check a projected expansion before allocating it; excess is `LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, without partial exposure changes. For DISPOSE, preflight the contingent-row count implied by the supplied finite attempts before enumerating execution slots, including when complete_attempt_inventory is false. Count the complete proposed replacement inventory plus other reservations' retained rows, with the originating reservation representing the first unresolved execution once. A projected count above 4096 is LIMIT_EXCEEDED and leaves governed state unchanged; exactly 4096 is admitted by this bound. Passing this preflight does not make an incomplete inventory complete or authorize its replacement. Numerical coefficients retain WCS2-004 limits. Policy, disposition, allowance and lifecycle application histories remain append-only without a fixed lifetime count, matching existing receipt/effect histories. Refusal reasons distinguish incomplete evidence, unresolved conflict, invalid policy, ineligible exception, incident/cumulative caps, pending evidence and changed basis. None converts an execution failure into permission.

Recovery admission follows existing schema/candidate/state/replay/revision precedence, then exact target/request/provider binding, retained known fact/attempt coverage, supplied authority/return binding and history structural admission. An omitted known fact or attempt, wrong provider, wrong request or mismatched target is `DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH`. Missing authenticated material is `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`. Invalid controller authority is `ADMINISTRATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED`. These refusals change no governed state. After admission, evaluate every applicable evidence/eligibility/policy/cap condition and sort the distinct reason codes. `complete_attempt_inventory:false` or missing affirmative past-and-future exclusion for requested no effect adds `RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE`; a covered conclusive contradiction without authoritative supersession adds `RECOVERY_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED`. For requested ESTABLISH_EFFECT, a conclusion other than EFFECT_ESTABLISHED adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE. A true rules_out_past_and_future_effects on that packet adds RECOVERY_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED. These conditions are evaluated independently and neither establishes an effect or clears a block. Missing effect-history corroboration adds `RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE` and the applicable existing history reason. For requested ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT, an unusable required history adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE and that history's applicable reason. It supplies no fabricated aggregate evaluation or release. Explicit LEAVE_UNRESOLVED retains its unusable-history exception. Missing, revoked, expired or superseded exception policy adds `RECOVERY_POLICY_INVALID`; ineligible uncertainty adds `RECOVERY_EXCEPTION_INELIGIBLE`; incident/cumulative failures add `RECOVERY_INCIDENT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`/`RECOVERY_ALLOWANCE_EXCEEDED`. A failed substantive condition retains `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED` with `WITHHELD`, bounded known charges and no allowance consumption. Pending target evidence equal to the complete packet being admitted can be consumed by that transition; other relevant pending evidence adds `RECOVERY_PENDING_EVIDENCE` and prevents clearance. Future permission with unclassified relevant pending evidence refuses `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`. Explicit LEAVE_UNRESOLVED grants nothing and retains a LEAVE_UNRESOLVED disposition without inventing a positive conclusion. Its receipt is DISPOSITION_RECORDED only when no applicable substantive reason remains; otherwise it is WITHHELD. A covered conclusive contradiction without authoritative supersession adds RECOVERY_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED, and a supplied native effect not attributed to the packet's attempts adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE, including for explicit LEAVE_UNRESOLVED. The exception for an unusable supplied history below is unchanged. An unusable supplied history is not adopted and adds no reason to an explicit `LEAVE_UNRESOLVED`.

For every requested registry disposition, a projectable native EFFECT lacking its prescribed record in a usable required history adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE and HISTORY_UNCORROBORATED. An unprojectable actual adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE; do not invent a prescribed record or a numerical amount. An unusable required history retains its existing applicable reasons for requested establishment or acceptance. Explicit LEAVE_UNRESOLVED retains R-i's exemption from reasons caused by the unusable history itself, but inability to assess a retained actual independently adds RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE. Withhold these dispositions, retain the evidence and remaining bounded possible-execution inventory, and grant no allowance or clearance. Do not add HISTORY_UNCORROBORATED merely because a history is unusable.
