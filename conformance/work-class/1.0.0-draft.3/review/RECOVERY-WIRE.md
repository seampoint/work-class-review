# Recovery wire agreement

Implementation agreement, October 7, 2026. These names become closed definitions in the existing draft 3 schemas. All added configuration and state members are optional for definitions that do not select recovery. A selected recovery registry starts with core.recovery={policies:[],dispositions:[],contingent:[],allowances:[]}; selected work starts with recovery_records:[]. Missing selected state or an unselected nonempty recovery ledger is invalid. Existing historical records remain unchanged.

## Shared names

`RecoveryTarget` is `{kind:"RESERVATION", registry_digest:DIGEST, reservation:ID}` or `{kind:"WORK_OUTCOME", instance_id:ID, occurrence_id:DIGEST, outcome_digest:DIGEST}`. Domain `recovery-target` hashes the whole target.

`RecoveryConfiguration` is `{provider:ID, source:ID, controller_role:ID, policy_owner_role:ID, policy_families:ID[], negative_prerequisite_behavior:"STOP", exception_profiles:("MONETARY_DUPLICATE_SEND")[]}`. It is optional `recovery` on the reservation registry configuration and work definition.

`RecoveryBasis` is `{id:ID, actor:ID, principal:ID, role:ID, kind:"HUMAN", scope_digest:DIGEST, operations:("ESTABLISH_EFFECT"|"ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT"|"RELEASE_DISPUTED_EXPOSURE"|"ISSUE_POLICY"|"REVOKE_POLICY")[], validity:interval, at:INSTANT, revoked:boolean, provider:ID, source:ID, evidence_ref:ID}`. Controller scope is the `recovery-target` digest. Policy-owner scope is the `recovery-policy-scope` digest of the complete policy scope below. Existing `adminAct` and `adminReturn` shapes are reused as new `recoveryAct` and `recoveryReturn` definitions, with operation kinds replaced by the recovery operation names. `RecoveryAuthorization` is `{act:recoveryAct, returned:recoveryReturn}`. Return act digest uses `recovery-act`; its canonical base64 bytes must decode to that exact act. The act subject uses `recovery-subject` over the complete source event after deleting payload.authorization and payload.controller for registry events, or top-level controller for a lifecycle DISPOSE. Event identity, expected revisions, clocks, packet and requested result remain bound.

`RecoveryHost` is `{caller:ID, recovery_bases:RecoveryBasis[], authenticated_records:{kind:"RECOVERY_EVIDENCE"|"RECOVERY_RETURN", record_digest:DIGEST, provider:ID, source:ID}[], pending_evidence:{target:RecoveryTarget, record_digest:DIGEST}[]}`. Reservation host has optional `recovery:RecoveryHost`; lifecycle step input has optional `recovery_host:RecoveryHost`. Pending attributable evidence blocks affected permission boundaries until admitted or retained classification establishes irrelevance. These host assertions require declared protected custody; they do not prove it.

`RecoveryAttempt` is `{id:ID, attempt_digest:DIGEST, executor:ID, connector:ID, request_digest:DIGEST, maximum_executions:NATURAL, effect_ids:ID[], no_effect:boolean}`. The request digest uses `reservation-native-request` over the complete packet request. Maximum executions is positive. Attempts are sorted by id and unique; their inventory must include every retained relevant attempt with its exact identity and bindings. The authenticated complete all-writer inventory may also establish additional attempts beyond those retained locally. Equal arguments alone do not supply a bound.

Source interpretation recorded October 7: a fresh complete authenticated all-writer guarantee may increase or reduce an attempt's finite execution upper bound. The attempt identifier, attempt digest, request, executor, connector and known actual-effect ownership remain bound across all retained packets. A reduction affirmatively excludes the removed possible slots; a caller estimate or partial inventory cannot do so. The changed bound changes the generation and requires an explicit superseding decision. Duplicate-send exception eligibility requires at least two total possible executions, not two attempt rows. One attempt bounded at two executions can qualify; one sole attempt bounded at one cannot.

`RecoveryEvidence` is `{id:ID, provider:ID, source:ID, record_type:"RECOVERY_ADJUDICATION", record_digest:DIGEST, evidence_ref:ID, provider_revision:NATURAL, target:RecoveryTarget, request:nativeRequest, covered_evidence_digests:DIGEST[], superseded_evidence_digests:DIGEST[], attempts:RecoveryAttempt[], conclusion:"EFFECT_ESTABLISHED"|"NO_EFFECT_ESTABLISHED"|"UNRESOLVED", native_outcomes:(reservation.native|lifecycle.nativeEvidence)[], rules_out_past_and_future_effects:boolean, complete_attempt_inventory:boolean}`. The packet digest uses `recovery-evidence`. Its provider/source must match the original selected reservation settlement/no-effect provider/source or target work completion provider/source, independently of the governance provider/source in RecoveryConfiguration. `recovery-fact` hashes the complete native record after removing only id, evidence_id, evidence_ref, record_digest and observed_at. Coverage digests are these normalized fact identities, not report identities. Coverage includes every relevant retained fact; supersession names each contradictory fact. The provider's authenticated packet asserts its authoritative adjudication relationship, not a caller-selected record. `generation_digest` uses `recovery-generation` over `{target, request, covered_evidence_digests, superseded_evidence_digests, attempts, conclusion, native_outcomes, rules_out_past_and_future_effects}` with native_outcomes replaced by normalized native fact projections, deduplicated and sorted by canonical bytes; covered/superseded arrays are sorted unique and attempts sort by id. The native projections omit the same five wrapper keys as recovery-fact. Other named preimage members retain their exact values. Packet/report wrappers, provider_revision, record type and packet identity do not enter this generation preimage. Report wrappers/id do not affect generation.

## Policy events and state

`RecoveryPolicyScope` is `{principal:ID, registry_digest:DIGEST, budget_anchors:ID[], work_classes:ID[], executors:ID[], connectors:ID[], unit:"USD", profile:"MONETARY_DUPLICATE_SEND", window_seconds:"86400"}`. Scope arrays are sorted and unique. `RecoveryPolicy` is `{id:ID, family:ID, version:NATURAL, scope:RecoveryPolicyScope, per_incident_limit:DECIMAL_STRING, cumulative_limit:DECIMAL_STRING, validity:interval}`. Limits are exact nonnegative canonical decimal strings. Policy identity uses `recovery-policy` over the complete policy. Family attribution remains stable across versions and limits; scope changes require migration and are refused.

Reservation event `RECOVERY_POLICY` retains the standard `{id,expected_revision,kind,clock,payload,administration:null}` envelope. Payload is `{action:"ISSUE"|"REVOKE", family:ID, policy:RecoveryPolicy|null, policy_digest:DIGEST|null, supersedes:DIGEST|null, authorization:RecoveryAuthorization}`. ISSUE needs the complete policy and exact preceding version in supersedes (null only initially). REVOKE names an exact current policy digest and includes its full policy for independently bound scope. Both require current independently selected policy-owner remit.

Reservation event `DISPOSE` uses the same envelope and `administration:null`. Payload is `{target:RecoveryTarget, requested_result:"ESTABLISH_EFFECT"|"ESTABLISH_NO_EFFECT"|"ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED"|"LEAVE_UNRESOLVED", evidence:RecoveryEvidence, controller:RecoveryAuthorization|null, policy_digest:DIGEST|null, supersedes:DIGEST|null, histories:reservation.history[]}`. Caller comes from RecoveryHost. Effect/no-effect needs controller; no-effect additionally needs RELEASE_DISPUTED_EXPOSURE in that same controller designation. Exception needs current policy and in-scope caller, no controller. Leave unresolved retains evidence and blocking without granting authority.

Registry `core.recovery` is `{policies:RecoveryPolicyRecord[], dispositions:RecoveryDisposition[], contingent:RecoveryContingent[], allowances:RecoveryAllowance[]}`. Policy record is `{policy, policy_digest, revoked:boolean, event_digest, authorization}`. Disposition is `{id:DIGEST, incident_digest:DIGEST, generation_digest:DIGEST, acceptance_basis_digest:DIGEST|null, target, result:requested_result, evidence, policy_digest:DIGEST|null, controller:RecoveryAuthorization|null, supersedes:DIGEST|null, event_digest:DIGEST, accepted_at:INSTANT, source_event:reservation.event}`. `id` uses `recovery-disposition` over `{incident_digest,generation_digest,result,policy_digest,controller,supersedes}`. Incident uses `recovery-incident` over `{principal,registry_digest,reservation,occurrence,error_class:"DUPLICATE_SEND_UNCERTAINTY"}` for reservation targets; work target uses `{principal,target}`. Acceptance basis uses `recovery-acceptance-basis` over `{incident_digest,generation_digest,policy_digest}`. A fresh event id with an existing identical basis retains no second disposition or cap/exposure charge only while that disposition's coverage and policy remain current. Returning to an earlier generation after supersession requires a fresh full-case acceptance against the current head. Its recurring basis digest does not refund or merge earlier allowance admissions.

Fresh acceptance reuse still admits, authenticates and evaluates every supplied current affected history. It retains usable complete prefixes and reconciles retracted prescribed events; ordinary native-fact replay supplies no exception to DISPOSE. Missing or non-prefix affected history refuses DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH; missing history authentication refuses EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE. Unavailable or stale complete rows cannot establish fresh reuse. Effect, no-effect and leave decisions have no fresh-ID acceptance reuse. An identical noncontradictory positive conclusion can be recorded again with the exact current predecessor and fresh bound authority. Explicit conservative LEAVE can also supersede a positive conclusion without undoing historical facts. For LEAVE over a NO_EFFECT_ESTABLISHED packet, provider selection remains the original declared no-effect pair; otherwise it uses the original settlement pair.

Contingent is `{id:DIGEST, disposition_digest:DIGEST, reservation:ID, attempt_id:ID, execution_index:NATURAL, anchor:ID, mapping:ID, occurrence:ID, key:typedValue[], value:typedValue}`. The anchor, mapping, occurrence, key and typed value are the exact existing reservation contribution projection. Identity uses `recovery-contingent` over `{registry_digest,reservation,attempt_id,execution_index,anchor,mapping,key}`. The first unresolved execution is represented by the original reservation; each additional unresolved execution has one contingent row. Actual established effects substitute exactly once; resolved contingent rows disappear from current inventory but remain reconstructible from retained dispositions/events.

Allowance is `{acceptance_basis_digest:DIGEST, policy_family:ID, budget:ID, actor_kind:"BUDGET"|"EXECUTOR"|"CONNECTOR", actor:ID, amount:DECIMAL_STRING, accepted_at:INSTANT}`. All distinct applicable identities charge the whole case amount once; these counters never multiply ordinary budget exposure. No refund on resolution. `recovery-allowance` may identify this row if an implementation needs one; array order is canonical bytes.

When a structurally valid bounded exception cannot pass policy/incident/cumulative/budget checks, retain a disposition with result LEAVE_UNRESOLVED and its original source_event requested_result ACCEPT_UNRESOLVED, retain all known bounded contingent exposure, and return receipt WITHHELD plus precise reasons. Its acceptance_basis_digest is null and it consumes no allowance. Missing/wrong controller authority refuses ADMINISTRATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED and grants nothing. Missing authenticated evidence refuses EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE; incompatible fields refuse DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH or INPUT_INVALID. A subsequent positive disposition may explicitly supersede a prior LEAVE_UNRESOLVED decision for the same generation. Only successful acceptance has a nonnull acceptance_basis_digest and receives reuse protection.

Reservation receipt optional `recovery_disposition_digest:DIGEST` and decisions add `POLICY_RECORDED`, `DISPOSITION_RECORDED`, `DISPOSITION_REUSED`. Refusals use existing INPUT_INVALID/DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH/REVISION_CONFLICT/EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE/ADMINISTRATION_NOT_ESTABLISHED. Retained withhold reasons add RECOVERY_AUTHORITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED, RECOVERY_EVIDENCE_INCOMPLETE, RECOVERY_CONFLICT_UNRESOLVED, RECOVERY_POLICY_INVALID, RECOVERY_EXCEPTION_INELIGIBLE, RECOVERY_INCIDENT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED, RECOVERY_ALLOWANCE_EXCEEDED, RECOVERY_PENDING_EVIDENCE and RECOVERY_BASIS_CHANGED; ordinary exposure/authority/history failures retain existing reasons.

## Lifecycle events and records

Lifecycle `DISPOSE` is `{schema,event_id,kind:"DISPOSE",instance_id,expected_state_revision,expected_state_digest,target,requested_result,evidence,controller,policy_digest:null,supersedes,clock_revision,activation_clocks}`. It supports WORK_OUTCOME, effect/no-effect/leave only. All shared fields are identical to reservation definitions. This event uses stepInput.recovery_host. No invented reservation or registry is created.

`RecoveryRegistrySelection` is `{state:reservation.state, state_digest:DIGEST, host_evidence:reservation.host}`. Lifecycle `APPLY_DISPOSITION` is `{schema,event_id,kind:"APPLY_DISPOSITION",instance_id,expected_state_revision,expected_state_digest,target,disposition_digest,registry:RecoveryRegistrySelection,clock_revision,activation_clocks}`. Selected registry state is current and verified; application cannot mutate its accounting. Optional step input `recovery_registries:RecoveryRegistrySelection[]` (maximum one) provides current selection at later permission boundaries. Existing budgetInputs can also provide that registry selection; no cached local clearance suffices.

Lifecycle state optional `recovery_records:RecoveryApplication[]`; application is `{event:DISPOSE|APPLY_DISPOSITION, host_evidence:RecoveryHost, disposition:RecoveryDisposition, before_projection:RecoveryProjection, after_projection:RecoveryProjection}`. Work-only disposition.source_event is lifecycle DISPOSE instead of reservation event. Projection is `{status,active,completed,obligations,deadlines,total_activations,total_actuations,step_counters,fanout_passes}` with exact existing state member shapes. Retaining both supports independent checkpoint reversal and revalidation. Historical outcomes keep disputed=true. Local effective facts derive only from currently covered admissible dispositions; records cannot remove old outcomes.

Prior work-packet attempt history belongs to its complete WORK_OUTCOME target, including outcome_digest. Another target in the same occurrence has its own disposition history. Every retained local dispatch attempt remains a mandatory exact id/digest/request/executor/connector tuple; authenticated additional all-writer attempts may extend that known inventory. Current factual ownership remains bound across the retained packets of the same target.

Application optional `registry_selections:RecoveryRegistrySelection[]` has exactly one member when present. It retains the exact nonempty `stepInput.recovery_registries` inventory used by that application and is omitted for empty/absent input. Restart re-supplies this retained selection while recomputing the local consequence. This preserves current budget corroboration for conclusive local work completed when a work-only prerequisite is restored.

Lifecycle decision event kinds add DISPOSE/APPLY_DISPOSITION and dispositions add DISPOSITION_RECORDED/DISPOSITION_APPLIED. Optional decision field `recovery_disposition_digest` binds the retained decision. Current-time permission checks recompute coverage, policy validity and pending-evidence blockers. Terminal states remain terminal; source-selected negative prerequisite stops ACTIVE only. Exact original effect bindings restore unproposed descendants; permits never revive, sent/possibly sent work never resends, deadlines retain their declared consequences, and joined routes never rewind.

For an unresolved local operation with multiple distinct actual executions and no unique original established native basis identifying its completion, REC-015 requires the full retained factual packet and unchanged local projection, with empty reasons/details and no completion, route or actuation. Work-only DISPOSE returns DISPOSITION_RECORDED; registry APPLY_DISPOSITION returns DISPOSITION_APPLIED. Previously completed prerequisite selection uses equality to its exact original normalized native fact; additional accepted executions remain retained, and packet order supplies no selection rule.

## Inventory and retention limits

Registry-native conclusions used for local completion convert to lifecycle native evidence exactly as REC-015 specifies: native `id` becomes `evidence_id`; `effect_id`, or `id` for no effect, becomes `native_operation_id`; provider/source/reference/request/actual fields/collections/exclusion flag come from the selected conclusion; record type comes from the original step's matching completion carrier; record digest comes from the recovery packet; event time is native `occurred_at`; observed time is disposition `accepted_at`. Derive the ordinary native-request and native-evidence digests over the resulting complete records. Preserve superseded historical outcomes without using them as the new completion basis.

A recovery packet has at most 4096 attempts, 4096 native outcomes, 4096 covered facts and 4096 superseded facts. Each attempt has at most 4096 established effect identities. One current recovery contingent inventory has at most 4096 rows. At most 64 budgets, work classes, actors, connectors, policy families or selected designations occur in one bounded scope. Policies, dispositions, allowances and lifecycle application histories remain append-only without a fixed lifetime count, matching existing receipt/effect histories. A consumer checks the 4096 projected contingent limit before expanding numeric execution bounds and returns LIMIT_EXCEEDED without partial accounting. Canonical numeric coefficients retain WCS2-004 bounds.
