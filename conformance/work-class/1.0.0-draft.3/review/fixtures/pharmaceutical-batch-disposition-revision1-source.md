# Pharmaceutical batch disposition and customer-transfer authorization

Source revision 1, drafted October 6, 2026. Status: provisional fictional business source for draft 3 development. Cedar Vector Manufacturing, the customer, people, records and provider capabilities below are fictional. This source does not describe Genezen's procedures, establish regulatory compliance or constitute an adopted specification. Industry review can correct its assumptions without blocking development.

## 01. Scope and sources

The manufacturer is Cedar Vector Manufacturing, a fictional U.S. contract development and manufacturing organization. Its Indiana site manufactures one adeno-associated viral-vector drug-substance batch, CV-BATCH-026, for the fictional product owner Alder Therapy Research. The selected product stage is commercial manufacturing of an intermediate intended for the owner's further processing. The manufacturer's quality disposition concerns its own manufacturing output. The owner retains final drug-product release. This procedure permits an administrative inventory-status update only. Production, physical movement, shipping, administration to a patient and final drug-product release require separate procedures.

FDA's November 2016 quality-agreement guidance recommends documenting the parties' manufacturing responsibilities and distinguishes contract-facility disposition from the owner's final release. It is nonbinding guidance; an agreement cannot remove either party's applicable regulatory duties. [FDA quality-agreement guidance, parts III and IV.B.1.a](https://www.fda.gov/media/86193/download)

The cited regulations establish quality-unit authority, written procedures, record review and investigation of unexplained discrepancies or specification failures within their applicable scope. Product-specific regulations can supplement the general rules. Their exact application to this fictional viral-vector drug-substance stage remains an assumption for regulatory review. The selected fictional procedure below requires these protections independently of that unresolved applicability question. [21 CFR 211.22](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-B/section-211.22), [21 CFR 211.192](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-211/subpart-J/section-211.192), [21 CFR 210.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-210/section-210.2)

All later clauses are selected fictional organization policy unless marked as a host premise or unresolved assumption. Assay choices, role titles, approval expiry, customer assent and transfer terminology are not asserted to be universal regulatory requirements.

## 02. Request and allocation of responsibility

Request CV-TRANSFER-041 seeks one update of batch CV-BATCH-026, product AAV-DS-04 revision 2, site CV-IN-01, from QUARANTINED to APPROVED_FOR_CUSTOMER_TRANSFER. The named customer is Alder Therapy Research, owner identifier ATR-US-01. The update creates no shipment order and changes no quantity, location, possession or temperature-control instruction. The initial inventory record is CV-INVENTORY-026 revision 11.

Quality Agreement CV-ATR-07 revision 3 binds Cedar and Alder, this site, product and manufacturing stage. It became effective September 1, 2026, with no scheduled end. A signed superseding revision or withdrawal makes it inapplicable to an undispatched request. Cedar's Head of Quality and Alder's Quality Director jointly own changes to this agreement. A revised agreement cannot retrospectively change a retained event or silently transfer an existing approval to a new allocation of responsibility.

The agreement assigns Cedar's human Quality Disposition Reviewer responsibility for approving or rejecting Cedar's manufacturing output. Alder's human Customer Quality Representative supplies assent to receive that output for further processing under this agreement. Customer assent is not final product release. Each party keeps its own regulatory responsibility. Neither the agent nor a model can occupy either human approval role.

## 03. Participants and authority records

The Manufacturing Record Owner assembles the complete manufacturing record. The Laboratory Record Owner supplies authenticated laboratory conclusions. The Quality Investigation Owner supplies deviation investigations and follow-up. The Quality Disposition Reviewer approves or rejects the exact dossier. The Customer Quality Representative records customer assent. The Inventory Records Agent performs the authorized inventory update. The Inventory Records Custodian investigates and corrects inventory records. The Head of Quality owns this procedure and Cedar's role-remit register; the customer Quality Director owns its customer's role-remit register.

Cedar's remit register CV-REMIT revision 4 authorizes the Quality Disposition Reviewer to decide this batch disposition and withdraw that decision. It authorizes the Inventory Records Agent to submit only the exact approved administrative update. The customer register ATR-REMIT revision 2 authorizes customer assent. Human approvers must have their stated role and remit when signing. The executor must have a current authenticated session and current Cedar role occupancy at proposal and dispatch. The executor cannot supply either human decision or change source records while performing this class.

The illustrative positive occurrence uses Cedar reviewer quality-reviewer-01, customer representative customer-quality-01 and executor inventory-agent-01. These are synthetic identities. Authenticity, role custody and truthful authoritative returns are host responsibilities. A valid digest or a plausible title does not establish those facts.

## 04. Exact dossier and prerequisite records

The manufacturing dossier CV-DOSSIER-026 revision 5 identifies the batch, product revision, site, customer, quality-agreement revision and complete evidence inventory. Each referenced record includes its provider, record type, identifier, immutable revision and retained content digest. A stable batch number or a link to a mutable document cannot identify the approved dossier.

The required evidence is the complete manufacturing record CV-MFG-026 revision 8; laboratory release panel CV-LAB-026 revision 6; deviation register CV-DEV-026 revision 4; batch-hold register CV-HOLD-026 revision 9; the product specification AAV-DS-04-SPEC revision 2; and customer assent ATR-ASSENT-026 revision 1. The manufacturing record must show all required operations and reviews complete. The laboratory panel must identify this batch and specification, include identity, vector-genome titer, product-specific potency, endotoxin and microbiological-safety conclusions, and show every required conclusion as PASS under its approved method. Raw measurements alone do not replace the laboratory's attributable conclusion. This source chooses no universal assay method or numerical release limit.

Every deviation needs an attributable human quality disposition, its investigation, conclusions and required follow-up. OPEN, UNINVESTIGATED or an unresolved failed specification blocks this update. A CLOSED_ACCEPTABLE deviation must identify the exact batch and the Quality Disposition Reviewer's authority. Such a decision cannot waive a nonwaivable requirement. Missing investigation evidence cannot be recast as an accepted deviation because an agent predicts the result would be acceptable.

The current hold register must establish that no blocking hold exists for this batch, product or site scope. A passing result for another batch, a different specification revision or a superseded manufacturing record fails the prerequisite. ABSENT is usable only when the authoritative query explicitly establishes absence for the required hold subject; an unavailable, stale, incomplete or contradictory response fails the gate.

## 05. Exact human decisions and changed evidence

Disposition approval CV-APPROVAL-026 revision 1 records APPROVE, the Cedar reviewer, signing time, exact dossier identity, request CV-TRANSFER-041, batch, site, product revision, customer, agreement revision, permitted old and new inventory status and executor role. Customer assent independently binds that same dossier and destination. A general customer relationship or an unsigned approval summary does not satisfy either decision.

For the illustrative positive occurrence, both decisions are effective at 2026-10-06T12:00:00Z. Approval expires exclusively at 2026-10-07T12:00:00Z, or earlier upon its attributable withdrawal or invalidation. The source-selected expiry is an organizational control. It does not permit reliance on subsequently invalidated evidence. Customer assent has the same interval and may be withdrawn by its customer owner.

A changed required evidence revision, laboratory conclusion, deviation disposition, specification, quality agreement, destination or transfer result invalidates the old dossier approval and customer assent before an undispatched update. A new dossier requires fresh human decisions. Recording a human decision, verifying it and executing the inventory update remain separate actions. Model scores, urgency and an earlier passing gate cannot substitute for a current decision.

## 06. Proposal, dispatch and the commit boundary

Before proposal, the agent verifies the exact request and current identities, the dossier and both human decisions, their role remits and effective intervals, current holds, current source revisions and absence of an earlier committed transfer under this business identity. The agent repeats every mutable check immediately before dispatch. Gate-time records must be queried for that instant, identify the exact subject, carry their authoritative revision and assert applicability at that instant. This fictional policy rejects cached substitutes.

The native request contains only request CV-TRANSFER-041, batch CV-BATCH-026, site CV-IN-01, product AAV-DS-04 revision 2, customer ATR-US-01, dossier revision 5 and digest, agreement revision 3, Cedar approval and customer-assent identities, expected inventory revision 11, old status QUARANTINED and new status APPROVED_FOR_CUSTOMER_TRANSFER. One operation makes this status change. No unit count or invented quantitative contribution is required.

Host premise: the Quality Gate Service maintains an authoritative revision covering the required evidence, approvals, assent, remits, agreement and holds. Every writer that changes a covered fact advances that revision and invalidates any affected authorization. The inventory provider conditionally checks that exact revision, approval effectiveness and expected inventory revision atomically with its update and event creation. A hold or withdrawal ordered before commit blocks the update. A recent check, short-lived token or local-only registry cannot supply this guarantee. If the host cannot coordinate all relevant writers at this boundary, it must withhold this profile and retain the missing capability as a gap.

The provider also enforces unique use of the request identifier and of the approved batch-disposition identity across all writers. Claims remain held after a possibly sent request or uncertain result. Restart, abandonment and approval expiry cannot create a new opportunity to transfer the same batch under the old approval. The host must retain authoritative provider history and transaction state before invoking the connector.

## 07. Native completion and uncertainty

The Inventory Ledger creates an attributable Batch Transfer Authorization Event. Its complete result identifies provider, source, record type, generated event identifier, event timestamp, native operation identifier, request, batch, site, product revision, customer, dossier digest and revision, agreement revision, approval, customer assent, previous and resulting inventory revisions, old and new status and the committed quality-gate revision. A completion event must match every required request value and show the resulting inventory revision as 12. Transport success alone is insufficient.

The agent queries authoritative history by request and approved batch-disposition identity. One exact event confirms completion of the administrative update only. No event, an unavailable or ambiguous lookup, a missing field or mismatched event leaves the effect unconfirmed. The current instance stops, retains its request, evidence and held claims, creates no automatic successor and permits no blind resend. The agent cannot restore the old inventory status merely because the response timed out.

A later exact event can establish the historical effect and consume its claims without reopening the stopped instance. Conflicting or additional events remain retained and go to investigation. A hold effective after commit creates a separate quality-containment duty: the Quality Investigation Owner promptly alerts Cedar's Head of Quality and the customer Quality Representative through the protected quality channel. The original event remains intact. Recall, physical containment and subsequent inventory changes require their own authorized procedures.

## 08. Referrals, correction and subsequent work

The agent retains a referral intent naming Cedar's Quality Review Queue for failed evidence, approval, agreement or hold checks. Inventory uncertainty also names the Inventory Records Custodian. Delivery records, recipient receipt and human resolution are separate host evidence. No referral grants authority, changes inventory or proves that the recipient acted.

An inventory correction requires the Custodian's current role and an exact correction authorization from Cedar's Head of Quality. The correction record identifies the original event and its digest, current expected revision, each old and new value, the authorizing decision, actor and timestamp. A change to batch, product, destination, disposition or approval basis additionally requires a new dossier, Cedar disposition and customer assent. Corrections append linked history; they never overwrite the original event or revive its permit. Provider history must establish the old attempt's effect before a new attempt can proceed. Unresolved uncertainty remains a block.

The agent cannot amend laboratory results, manufacturing records, quality agreements or regulatory obligations. Their named owners act through their separate procedures. A later authorized instance uses a new request, exact corrected evidence and fresh approval basis; it must independently satisfy every prerequisite and duplicate check.
