# Supplier bank change and invoice payment: proposed source revision 2

October 7, 2026. Status: provisional fictional source for recovery design, not adopted policy, an executable specimen, or evidence of verification. This revision proposes additions to [supplier-payment source revision 1](../../specimens/supplier-payment/source-policy.json). Revision 1 and its generated specimen remain unchanged. The baseline candidate is `sha256:6add48c901264a8e5fa95286e259881fae215a8c38cc78d7b3dfccd50c6fdcf1`; it does not implement these additions. The [recovery proposal](../../../../../planning/work-packages/WORK-CLASS-DRAFT3-RECOVERY-PROPOSAL.md) defines the intended portable design and decision brief.

## 01. Organization, operations and original obligations

The fictional principal remains `sample-buying-organization`. The work class remains supplier bank change followed by invoice payment: change the exact supplier destination, then pay the exact invoice to the established destination. There is no new business operation after payment. The source example uses `supplier-17`, `acct-new`, the supplier master provider and the payment provider already declared by revision 1. Recovery creates no payment, changes no destination and does not compensate a completed payment.

All seven revision 1 obligations remain: exact payment authority; the 1,000 USD supplier ceiling over the rolling 86,400-second window; independent confirmation of the exact new destination; exact supplier-master authority; attributable establishment of that master-data effect before payment; retained uncertain effects; and separation between the confirmation actor and payment-release actor. The complete initial journal still contains 800 USD of committed supplier exposure. Ordinary unknown outcomes retain their reservation and accept later matching evidence under the existing contract. A 150 USD pending payment leaves 50 USD of ordinary capacity; another 150 USD remains forbidden. Recovery cannot waive that ceiling or these obligations.

## 02. Recovery authority and evidence custody

The organization designates a human `payment-recovery-controller` through its protected governance authority provider. The designation explicitly covers the named work instance, registry where applicable, exact outcome or reservation, disposition kind, evidence set and validity interval. For payment adjudication, the controller must differ from the affected executor and human payment releaser. For a supplier-master outcome, the controller needs an explicit supplier-master remit and must differ from that outcome's executor and destination-confirmation actor. There is no requirement to name a future unselected payment releaser to adjudicate the master-data prerequisite. Registration authority alone supplies no recovery remit.

For disputed no-effect release, the controller's exact acceptance is the organizational release act for the proposed disposition operation. Its independently selected designation must expressly authorize both adjudication and release of that exact disputed exposure, and the act binds the target, evidence, covered attempts and released contributions. No second budget-administrator act is required for this new path. A controller with adjudication-only remit cannot release exposure; an ordinary RELEASE act alone cannot dispose of a dispute. Ordinary release under revision 1 remains unchanged.

The organization separately designates a human `payment-recovery-policy-owner` through the same protected governance authority provider. That designation expressly permits issuing, superseding and revoking exception policies for the named principal, registry, policy family, budget, work-class and executor/connector scope, units, eligibility, limits and validity. Each policy act binds the complete version and those terms; withdrawals name the exact policy or family. Neither an executor nor an administrator designation can supply this remit by inference. The same human may occupy the policy-owner and controller roles only with separate valid designations; the controller's target-specific independence still applies. A policy-owner act authorizes deterministic application within its bounds, without a further case-specific human acceptance.

Provider evidence must be authenticated independently by the host and attributable to the original operation, exact request and complete attempt inventory. A correction or adjudication record identifies the full conflicting evidence set, the authoritative final conclusion, each rejected or superseded conclusion and its reason, and the provider revision establishing that relationship. The controller authorizes a disposition of that evidence. Neither the controller, a model assessment nor an agent assertion makes unsupported facts true. If the provider cannot supply the required authoritative relationship, the fact remains unresolved.

No-effect evidence must exclude both past and future execution of every outstanding attempt for the exact original invocation. A cancellation request, empty lookup, timeout, withdrawn grant or expired permit is insufficient. An effect conclusion must identify every established execution, its exact result, native identity, occurrence time and corresponding complete payment history where applicable. A second actual payment remains an additional payment even when its fields equal the first.

## 03. Establish an effect

The controller may authorize an effect disposition only when the selected authoritative evidence resolves the exact disputed conclusion. The retained request and result preserve all original and conflicting records. Payment accounting includes each actual native effect once, removes only uncertainty that the evidence closes and records any unresolved residual exposure. Evidence resolving one attempt cannot close another attempt.

For the first recovery specimen, restoration of work that depended on a completed supplier-master change requires the authoritative conclusion to establish the same supplier and destination values used by the original descendant's prior-effect bindings. A different established destination cannot repair those bindings. It requires separately authorized work under a new instance, subject to any unresolved old invocation. Historical completion, routes and permits remain recorded as they occurred.

## 04. Establish no effect

The controller may authorize release of disputed payment exposure only after the provider establishes no past or future effect for the exact covered attempt set. Release applies only to that covered exposure. Any actual charge, uncovered attempt, unrelated dispute or held exclusive identifier remains governed by its own rule. The original operation follows its declared no-effect failure behavior; no-effect disposition does not make it successful.

A new payment after established no effect needs a new instance, invoice-payment occurrence and current authority. Its provider evidence must link the prior invocation and establish that it cannot execute later. This source selects no automatic retry. If a formerly completed supplier-master change is established not to have occurred, old dependent work cannot resume using that claimed change. Applying that negative disposition stops an affected ACTIVE instance without rewriting its earlier completion or route, retains any payment exposure and creates no successor. An already STOPPED or COMPLETE instance retains its terminal status. Separately authorized replacement work must re-establish the required prerequisite.

## 05. Pre-approved bounded uncertainty

The designated policy owner may issue a versioned, time-bounded and revocable human preapproval for an operational duplicate-send uncertainty involving unchanged payment arguments. It names the principal, registry, policy family, budget, allowed work class, eligible connector and executor scope, validity interval, applicable limits and the policy-owner authority. The authenticated caller must be within the policy's application scope. The deterministic consumer checks current policy validity, complete eligible evidence, upper-bound exposure and every applicable limit; no case-specific human acceptance is required. The preapproval does not authorize another send of the uncertain payment.

The first proposed profile covers nonnegative monetary SUM exposure in USD only. Its illustrative limits are 5 USD of total unresolved case exposure per acceptance and 10 USD of accepted exposure during a rolling 86,400-second window for each affected budget, each named executor and each named connector. All applicable limits must pass in the same transaction. These amounts are proposed fictional policy for the specimen, not an approved enterprise risk tolerance. The acceptance window is `(now - 86400 seconds, now]` using the registry's accepted governance clock. Exact replay consumes no allowance. Resolving an accepted incident does not refund its allowance within that window.

An incident has one stable identity bound to the principal, registry, originating reservation occurrence and eligible error class. Its evidence generation covers the complete relevant native facts and attempt inventory, independent of request wrappers and duplicate evidence identities. An acceptance additionally binds the exact policy act. A fresh event identifier for the same incident, generation and policy returns a reference to the existing decision and changes no exposure or allowance. Competing incompatible dispositions for the same generation are refused. Partial overlapping attempt sets cannot split one incident into smaller acceptances. New relevant facts or a newly authorized policy version require a current revision-checked decision, explicit supersession and full-case evaluation. A fresh accepted basis consumes its full current unresolved amount once; earlier allowance entries are not refunded. This conservative rule prevents renaming, splitting or policy-version changes from resetting the cumulative limit.

Eligible uncertainty has a finite authenticated attempt inventory, unchanged exact request fields and an attributable upper bound on executions per attempt. For the initial example, two distinct possibly sent attempts of the same 2 USD request can each cause at most one effect under the declared connector/provider guarantee. Total unresolved exposure is therefore 4 USD. Without that bound, uncertainty remains ineligible. An exact duplicate evidence report of the same attempt is deduplicated by existing rules and consumes no exception allowance.

Every possible execution remains charged. Retain the original reservation once and separately retain any incremental contingent exposure for additional possible executions. An authenticated idempotency guarantee can collapse attempts only when it covers these exact requests, identities and every relevant writer. Shared arguments or a token without such a guarantee cannot do so. The 5 USD incident limit measures the whole unresolved case, not merely its incremental duplicate amount. A 5 USD original plus a possible additional 5 USD execution is 10 USD and fails that limit.

The preapproval can permit otherwise eligible independent work in an affected partition. It cannot establish a supplier-master change, discharge the uncertain payment as successful, release its exposure, waive the 1,000 USD ceiling or reuse its consumed dispatch permission. Changed arguments, contradictory conclusive evidence, foreign effects, unknown grouping, unbounded executions, unavailable required history and nonmonetary reservation exceptions are outside this first profile. They retain their applicable blocks. Actor and connector cap attribution charges the full accepted case amount to each distinct involved executor and connector; these cap counters do not duplicate the ordinary budget exposure.

## 06. Revocation, expiry and later evidence

Preapproval expiry or revocation prevents new acceptances. It also ends the accepted incident's continuing exception clearance for future proposals and dispatches. The host must establish current validity at those boundaries; existing results and charges remain retained. A previously dispatched operation remains a historical fact and still needs reconciliation. An exhausted allowance prevents a new exception, without cancelling an otherwise still-valid previously accepted one.

New evidence outside a disposition's exact covered set invalidates its clearance until it is re-evaluated. A matching attributable effect may reduce uncertainty only through the ordinary admissible reconciliation or a fresh disposition covering the expanded evidence. A late effect after no-effect release restores conservative exposure and blocks the affected work; the earlier release stays in history. A changed request, contradictory conclusion or additional unknown attempt cannot be hidden inside an old acceptance.

## 07. Application to work and other instances

One disposition commits its affected registry accounting, exception counters, retained decision and event-log record atomically. A work-only supplier-master dispute has no invented payment reservation; its disposition is committed against that work instance's current evidence and revision. Other instances sharing a registry apply an effective registry disposition against their own current evidence and revision before their local dependency blocks change. Independent registry work need not wait for an unrelated instance to apply the decision.

Restore only work blocked solely by a now-established original factual basis. For unproposed payment, use the completed ancestor's retained native fact and the definition's declared supplier/destination bindings; for an issued-permit occurrence, preserve that permit's exact prior-effect bases. Re-evaluate current authority, actor separation, budgets, clocks, deadlines and dispatch eligibility before any new action. An unproposed payment can become eligible for a fresh proposal; a blocked payment with an issued permit cannot reuse that permit and requires a fresh current renewal if the original unchanged occurrence is still safely dispatchable. A sent, possibly sent, consumed, expired or superseded dispatch authorization never revives. Changed arguments require new work.

Unresolved exceptions cannot restore descendants that require proof of the uncertain effect. Completed occurrences and already joined routes are never rewound. A deadline that expired while blocked follows its already declared expiry consequence when the block is safely reconsidered; recovery grants no deadline extension. A permanently stopped or completed instance remains terminal. When evidence is still unresolved, retain the blocked work and its reason. When authoritative evidence establishes that the original prerequisite was false or materially different, apply the specific instance stop selected in section 04. This source selects no general cancellation or compensation mechanism.

## 08. Retention, replay and refusal

Retain the exact candidate, source revision, work and registry identities, covered records, native evidence and history revisions, authority acts, expected revisions, requested result, before-and-after exposure and blocks, exception accounting and application receipts. The same request identity and exact body return the retained result without another charge or action. Changed bytes under that identity are refused. A new request against stale state or changed evidence is refused for fresh evaluation.

The host protects incoming evidence even if application fails, supplies authentic actors, providers and clocks, prevents stale-state selection and atomically persists the intended registry/work/log changes before any new dispatch. Attributable relevant evidence awaiting admission or classification prevents affected proposals and dispatches from relying on old clearance, including after a failed state write. Its target-bound pending inventory participates in the permission boundary until admission or a retained classification establishes irrelevance. Malformed or unattributable input does not by itself block unrelated work. Process memory, a digest or a fixture declaration does not prove these guarantees. Development needs success and refusal examples for each disposition, authority failures, complete and partial evidence, shared-instance propagation, cap contention, write rollback, restart and renewed conflict. Those examples have not been executed for this proposed revision.
