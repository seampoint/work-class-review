# Bank suspicious-activity filing and separate customer communication

Source revision 1, drafted October 6, 2026. Status: provisional fictional business source for draft 3 development. Maple Research Bank, its customer, identities, records and gateway capabilities are fictional. This source establishes no Hummingbird capability, bank endorsement or regulatory compliance. Any executable apparatus must use synthetic records and synthetic endpoints, with no real filing or customer message. Industry review can correct assumptions without blocking development.

## 01. Scope and public obligations

This procedure concerns one fictional U.S. bank, one opened investigation, one retained human reporting decision and one initial Suspicious Activity Report (SAR). The suspect was identified on the date of initial detection. Joint filings, continuing reports, criminal determinations, account closure, freezes and changes to customer funds are outside this first operation. A separately governed customer message may request an explanation of underlying transactions without revealing the SAR's existence.

For banks, the cited rule governs SAR filing, specifies the initial-detection clock, requires five-year retention from filing and restricts disclosure of SAR information. Its identified-suspect filing period is 30 calendar days. The possible additional period for identifying an unknown suspect does not apply to this selected case. Situations requiring immediate attention have an additional immediate law-enforcement notification duty. [31 CFR 1020.320(b), (d) and (e)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-31/subtitle-B/chapter-X/part-1020/subpart-C/section-1020.320)

The September 2, 2026 joint statement permits communication of underlying facts without revealing that a SAR exists. Other privacy and bank-policy restrictions remain applicable. [FinCEN and banking agencies' joint statement](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2026-09/Joint-Statement-on-SAR-Confidentiality.pdf)

The April 2026 filing instructions permit a limited CSV attachment, distinguish supporting records from transmitted content, describe complete corrected/amended reports and explain that the guidance's 90/120-day continuing-report schedule is optional. This case selects no filing attachment and no continuing report. [FinCEN SAR filing instructions, sections 6.1.3, 6.1.4 and 6.1.6](https://bsaefiling.fincen.gov/resources/FinCENSARFilingInstructions.pdf)

All later clauses are selected fictional bank policy unless marked as a host premise or unresolved assumption. Human approval roles, separation rules, review thresholds, reminder times and gateway behavior are not asserted to be universal federal requirements.

## 07. Receipt, rejection, uncertainty and correction

The gateway retains distinct dispatch, transport acknowledgement, processing acknowledgement and report-disposition records. A matching completion record identifies its provider, source, record type, generated event identifier, event timestamp, bank, case, package identity, submission token, native submission identity, disposition, processing warnings/errors and the BSA Identifier when assigned. The exact provider-specific disposition that establishes accepted filing must be declared and checked. An HTTP response, queued job or merely received acknowledgement is not sufficient.

A confirmed accepted filing establishes the historical filing effect and consumes the initial-report claim. It does not certify investigation accuracy or regulatory compliance. A reported rejection preserves the response and its exact processing stage. Only qualified evidence that no filing occurred can support a separately authorized new submission after correction. No rejection label alone proves that absence.

A timeout, ambiguous acknowledgement, missing receipt, unavailable lookup or conflicting receipt leaves the effect unknown. The instance stops further submission, retains the package, consumed submission token and unresolved business claim, and refers the result to the Reporting Records Custodian and Bank Secrecy Act Officer through the protected reporting channel. The agent must not blindly resend, start a replacement initial filing or declare success. A later matching receipt can reconcile historical effect without reviving the old dispatch permission.

After a confirmed filing, a correction requires a new full corrected/amended package, fresh assessment and approval, an explicit reference to the prior filing and its known BSA Identifier, and a new submission token. The original filing remains retained. It cannot be silently overwritten or treated as an unfiled draft. The Officer selects the correct report type and handles unknown prior identifiers under the reviewed filing instructions. The host permits access only to the named reporting roles with current case scope and appropriate purpose. This internal access policy does not authorize external SAR sharing beyond applicable law.
