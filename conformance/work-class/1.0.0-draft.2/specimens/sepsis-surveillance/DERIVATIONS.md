## 4. Digest and identity constructions used

Occurrence identity: `digest("occurrence", {specification_pin, work_class_digest, instance_id, step_id, ordinal, predecessor_occurrence_ids, enclosing, object_key})`. Obligation identity: `digest("obligation", {kind, construct_id, pass, source_occurrence_ids, branch_id, object_key, join_step_id})`. Deadline activation: `digest("deadline-activation", {deadline_id, occurrence_id, trigger_digest, source, activation_revision, activation_clock_revision, activation_instant, activation_clock_evidence_digest, due, boundary})`, where `due` is the activation instant plus the duration for `ELAPSED_DURATION` and the anchor occurrence's completed-row `occurred_at` plus the duration for `ELAPSED_FROM_ANCESTOR`; the anchor's identity is not a separate digest field. Authority `failed_checks` rows: the four construction-table fields of the failed check plus `reason`, sorted by canonical bytes.

Deadline collection order (LIFE-019 "by due instant, occurrence bytes and deadline identifier"; LIFE-014 orders the same rows "by due instant, canonical occurrence bytes and deadline identifier", and LIFE-019 orders active and completed occurrences by canonical occurrence bytes): the second key is the canonical bytes of the occurrence record the deadline row names, not the UTF-8 bytes of its `occurrence_id`. Before step 4 no two deadlines in this specimen shared a due instant, so the builder's earlier identifier-string key produced the same order and the error was latent. From revision 15 of the valid trace, `labs-hour-one` and `antibiotic-window` are both due 09:00. The antibiotic occurrence's canonical bytes begin `{"enclosing":[]` and the labs occurrence's begin `{"enclosing":[{`; `]` (0x5D) precedes `{` (0x7B), so `antibiotic-window` precedes `labs-hour-one`. Recorded 2026-09-13 after the first comparison run showed both consumers placing the rows in this order; the reading above is taken from the two paragraphs, and the builder was corrected to it. Permit, dispatch, outcome and decision identities omit their own identifier field. Work-state core, work-state, replay and transaction digests follow LIFE-019 as in the access-card builder. Authority checks follow the AUTHORITY-CHECKS table with `REVOCATION` queries for the root at every act instant and at evaluation, each act's capacity at its instant and at evaluation, the envelope and each credential at evaluation, deduplicated by subject and instant.
