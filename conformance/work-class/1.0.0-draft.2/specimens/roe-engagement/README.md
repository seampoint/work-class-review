This specimen is a synthetic scenario. It makes no claim about any weapons system, doctrine, the lawfulness of any engagement or the accuracy of any perception model. It governs the act after nomination, as the sepsis specimen governs the act after the alert. It implies no endorsement by the Software Engineering Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, Andrew Mellinger or the Department of Defense. It specifies constraint enforcement and traceability for one engagement decision and adds no targeting capability. It is a non-normative application of `seampoint.work-class/1.0.0-draft.2`; a general rule it exposes is a contract issue recorded in `EXPOSED-RULES.md`, not a change to this specimen.
