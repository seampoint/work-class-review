# Mapping: SEI reference architecture to the work-class contract

Status: non-normative design record for the `roe-engagement` specimen under `seampoint.work-class/1.0.0-draft.2`. The scenario is synthetic. It makes no claim about any weapons system, doctrine, the lawfulness of any engagement or the accuracy of any perception model. It implies no endorsement by the Software Engineering Institute, Carnegie Mellon University, Andrew Mellinger or the Department of Defense. It specifies constraint enforcement and traceability for the act after nomination and adds no targeting capability.

Sources: Mellinger, Heim, Schellenberg, Newman, Brooks and Loughin, *Reference Architecture for Assuring Ethical Conduct in LAWS*, SEI, April 2025, DOI 10.1184/R1/28700720, Distribution Statement A (cited below as RA with section numbers); Mellinger, Brooks, Fairfax and Justice, *CaTE Guidebook for the Development and TEVV of LAWS to Promote Trustworthiness*, SEI, April 2025, DOI 10.1184/R1/28701104, Distribution Statement A (cited as GB). File hashes are recorded in `review/design-input/SPECIMEN-SET-DIRECTION.md`.

## 2. Synthetic source policy

The policy is built from the rules-of-engagement example in RA 2.3.1, which the architecture takes from the Operational Law Handbook. Paraphrased: a unit may open fire only when it, friendly forces, or persons or property under its protection are threatened with deadly force, which includes an individual who fires or aims a weapon at them or otherwise demonstrates an intent to attack imminently. RA 2.3.1 derives four requirements from that rule: the system is configurable for predefined principles; it keeps traceability of how principles are ingested, stored and used at runtime; it requires user direction when a situation diverges from the principles and seeks a safe state without that direction; and it communicates how it decided. The nine obligations below are the specimen's synthetic reading of that text. They are the `source.obligations` of the work class and the `authority-source` obligations of the grant. `source-policy.json` carries the same text.

| Id | Obligation | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| `roe-01-deadly-force-threat` | Fire may be proposed against a track only when the observation record shows the track fires at, aims a weapon at, or otherwise demonstrates an intent to imminently attack the unit, friendly forces, or persons or property under designated protection. The declared intent values are `AIMS_WEAPON_AT_FRIENDLY`, `FIRES_AT_FRIENDLY` and `DEMONSTRATES_IMMINENT_ATTACK`; any other value withholds the proposal. | RA 2.3.1 rule a and (1) |
| `roe-02-protected-status-bars-fire` | A track whose protected status is other than `NONE` is never a permitted subject. | RA 4.3, protected entities marked as an invalid target; a synthetic extension, since RA 2.3.1 (1) names protected persons as parties whose endangerment justifies fire, not as barred targets |
| `roe-03-declared-threat-class-and-confidence` | The nominated track carries a declared threat class (`ARMED_ADULT` or `ARMED_QUADRUPED`) with classification confidence at or above 0.9. A lower confidence or an undeclared class withholds the proposal until a fresh observation is proposed. | RA 2.3.1 requirement 1; RA 5.7.2 confidence thresholds; RA Appendix A classes |
| `roe-04-rules-held-as-one-admitted-grant` | The rules of engagement are one reusable grant issued by the engagement commander at an exact revision, valid for the mission window and withdrawable through the command revocation source. A withdrawn or expired grant withholds every later proposal and leaves committed engagements and their evidence in place. | RA 2.3.1 requirement 1; RA 5.7.2 |
| `roe-05-operator-decision-per-engagement` | Every engagement requires an accepted per-instance decision by the engagement authority operator on the exact nominated track, organizationally attested. The resulting permit authorizes at most one dispatch of the exact request and expires 120 seconds after the proposal clock; after expiry a fresh proposal and a fresh operator decision are required. | RA 2.1.3; RA 5.5.7; RA 2.4.1 finding 1 |
| `roe-06-direction-or-safe-state` | When a nomination diverges from these rules the proposal is withheld and the operator's direction is required. If no direction produces a permitted engagement within ten minutes of the occurrence's activation on the mission clock, the engagement occurrence stops and no dispatch may follow. | RA 2.3.1 requirement 3; GB 3.3.13 |
| `roe-07-engagement-count-budget` | At most three engagements per mission may be committed or reserved within any rolling one-hour window on the mission clock, counted across every platform sharing the mission budget and including reserved engagements whose effect is not yet established. | RA 5.7.2; RA 2.1.4; synthetic bound |
| `roe-08-effect-established-only-by-assessment` | An engagement completes only on attributable battle damage assessment evidence for the exact dispatched request. A dispatch acknowledgement is not an effect. An unavailable assessment keeps the engagement open and its reservation charged. An assessment naming another entity, or conflicting evidence, is retained as disputed and blocks further engagements on the affected budget partition. | RA 5.5.8; RA 4.6 |
| `roe-09-runtime-traceability` | Every rule ingestion, proposal, permit, dispatch observation, effect observation and clock is retained as a record bound by digest to its predecessors, and every decision carries its reason codes so the record shows how the system decided. | RA 2.3.1 requirements 2 and 4; GB 3.3.14 |

Correspondence treatment: obligations 01 to 08 map to definition provisions in `correspondence-record.json`. Obligation 09 is carried by the protocol (LIFE-006 receipts, LIFE-019 state integrity, WCS2-005 readback), not by a definition provision, so it is listed under `uncovered_source_obligation_ids` with an `EXCLUDED` residue row that says so. The correspondence review, source confirmation and policy decision are labelled synthetic proxy acts; no person has performed them.

## 3. Observation declarations

The fields below are the specimen's declaration of what the blackboard supplies to the proposal. Each is a typed field of the `engage-track` step; the condition column names the envelope condition that reads it. Provenance and freshness of these values are not carried by the contract (exposed rule E8, a draft 3 design input; AUTH-001 now states the limitation); the host supplies them.

| Field | Type | Produced by (RA) | Condition | Bound or domain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `track_id` | `t.identity` | path tracker, entity registrar (5.5.2, 5.5.3) | scope subject binding | any nonempty identity |
| `mission_id` | `t.identity` | mission goals (5.7) | scope resource constant | `mission-1` |
| `classification` | `t.label` | object detector, classifier (5.5.1, Appendix A) | `roe-classification-is-declared-threat` | member of {`ARMED_ADULT`, `ARMED_QUADRUPED`} |
| `confidence` | `t.confidence` (DECIMAL, nonnegative) | classifier (Appendix A) | `roe-confidence-at-or-above-bound` | GTE `0.9` |
| `protected_status` | `t.label` | scene understanding (5.5.5) | `roe-target-not-protected` | EQUAL `NONE` |
| `demonstrated_intent` | `t.label` | activity recognizer (5.5.4) | `roe-demonstrated-intent-present` | member of {`AIMS_WEAPON_AT_FRIENDLY`, `DEMONSTRATES_IMMINENT_ATTACK`, `FIRES_AT_FRIENDLY`} |

## 4. Appendix A reuse

RA Appendix A describes the SEI's ROS-based proxy tests. The specimen reuses these elements so its cases correspond to tests the SEI already wrote.

| Appendix A element | Specimen use |
|---|---|
| Mission scenario: the system requests permission to engage Target C, the participant approves, the system engages and produces a report | The valid trace: proposal with an operator `INSTANCE_DECISION`, permit, dispatch, assessment evidence, completion |
| Mission scenario: the system identifies Target D and emits a warning; the participant decides whether the situation de-escalates | Variant 5: demonstrated intent absent, proposal withheld, operator direction required, deadline stop without direction |
| Requirement 7.4, request approval from the user when appropriate | The `DECIDE` gate on every engagement |
| Requirement 7.8, reports about engagement with targets | Decision receipts and the effect outcome record |
| Requirement 5.3, autonomous and manual kill chain modes | The specimen governs the manual mode only; the direction limits the specimen to the act after nomination |
| Classes armed adult, unarmed adult, child, robot quadruped | The `classification` domain: `ARMED_ADULT` and `ARMED_QUADRUPED` are declared threat classes; any other label withholds |
| Controller commands `CMD_ENGAGE` and `CMD_NO_GO` | The native request `engage-track` on interface `fire-control`; the stop after deadline expiry is the specimen's no-go |
| "In the event of uncertainty as defined by the conduct governance system, the TB4 defaulted to inaction" | Withholding on a false condition and the explicit stop on deadline expiry |

## 5. Claim boundary restated

The specimen governs the act after nomination. It shows that the draft-2 contract can carry the constraints in RA 2.3.1 and the traceability in RA 2.3.1 and GB 3.3.14 without domain vocabulary in the contract. It does not claim that the synthetic values, thresholds or intent labels are correct doctrine, that any real system behaves this way, or that a perception model can supply these fields at any accuracy. Every judgment in `DERIVATIONS.md` was derived from the specification text before either implementation ran.
