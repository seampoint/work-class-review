# Direct business authority

Status: `DRAFT_NOT_ADOPTED`, `APPARATUS_ONLY`. This normative candidate document defines a finite direct-grant profile. A successful authority result is `READY_FOR_RESERVATION`; it never establishes dispatch, successful effects, completion or satisfaction of a shared budget.

## AUTH-001. Complete subjects and explicit limits

An authority input contains exactly the selected specification pin, source, work_class, envelope, proposal, clock, host_selection, occupancies, capacities, credentials, acts, returns and observations. `authority.schema.json` defines their closed fields. Every supplied record is complete. References do not fetch additional records. All arrays that name records sort by their unique id in UTF-8 byte order, except acts sort by canonical act digest and authenticated-record tuples sort by their complete canonical bytes. A duplicate inventory key is invalid even when the two bodies are identical. Source obligations, role lists, operation lists, required credential kinds, criterion identifiers and shared-budget anchors are sorted unique inventories.

The source has an id, revision and complete supplied obligation texts. The evaluator checks identities and declared obligation references; it does not interpret those texts or establish source completeness or fidelity. The work class has an id, revision, nominal scalar types and steps. Each step declares its operation, interface, executor role, exact named fields, subject/resource field coordinates and required credential kinds. Types and values have the aggregate contract's scalar semantics, scoped here by the complete work-class digest. Proposal fields are exactly the declared fields for its step, with exact nominal types. There is no omission default. The proposal binds the work-class digest and stable id, instance, occurrence, step, operation, interface, executor and all admitted fields. The first occurrence is explicit, such as `issue:1`; another instance or occurrence changes the proposal subject. The host must establish that these complete fields equal the actual native request.

Digest kinds are `authority-source`, `authority-work-class`, `authority-envelope`, `authority-proposal`, `authority-operation`, `authority-root`, `authority-capacity`, `authority-occupancy`, `authority-credential`, `authority-act`, `authority-return`, `authority-observation`, `authority-evidence` and `authority-result`. Each hashes the complete corresponding record under WCS2-002. None includes its own digest. The operation subject is exactly `{envelope_digest,work_class_digest,proposal_digest}`. Those complete dependencies are retained in the input; no policy-bearing proposal field is excluded.

The envelope binds its exact source and work-class digests, supporting obligation identifiers, responsible principal, grantor/executor/organizational-attester roles and expected grant act id. Its eight operative members are scope, operations, limits, conditions, enforcement, escalation, revocation and temporal_validity. Gate and unsupported limitations are additional required members. No role name supplies a missing member.

Scope contains an explicit scope id and nonempty subject/resource selector sets. CONSTANT copies a nonempty identity string; BINDING names one declared binding. A binding names its step, exact proposal field and IDENTITY or STRING type. It stores no instance value. Every binding applies to every envelope operation, so a parameterized envelope supports exactly one step. A constant-scope envelope may support several. The resolved scope is the sorted unique union of exact selector values. No normalization, case conversion or inferred requester identity occurs. Each step's scope_fields explicitly names the admitted proposal fields identifying its actual subjects and resources. They resolve to nonempty IDENTITY/STRING values, producing actual_scope. Every actual subject and resource must belong to the corresponding resolved grant set. A constant grant for card-A cannot authorize a native request for card-B merely because the returned receipt repeats card-A.

Operations are exact step/operation/interface triples, one per step, admitted against the supplied work class. Conditions are named predicates over the proposed step's admitted fields using AGG-003's complete FIELD/LITERAL predicate rules. All branches are type-checked, including false or unused branches. Each condition declares its applicable step. Observation-based general predicates, arbitrary callbacks and English conditions remain unsupported; a source requiring them must retain that limitation and cannot obtain readiness through a field-only substitute. Conditions on the time elapsed since another occurrence and freshness predicates on proposal fields are among them in this candidate.

Limits contain per_action numerical upper bounds and shared_budgets. A per-action bound names its step, field, exact typed nonnegative bound and LT or LTE; the referenced field is a nonnegative INTEGER or DECIMAL of the same nominal type. Every operation allowed by this envelope requires either a per-action upper bound or at least one declared shared-budget anchor. The latter remains an outstanding reservation obligation, not a satisfied authority check. The reservation consumer must establish every required anchor's coverage of this exact operation and jointly reserve its contribution before permission. A reference to an unrelated mapping or one that selects no contribution cannot satisfy that coverage requirement.

Enforcement declares mechanism identifiers, safe state and pre-actuation response. This profile supports only AUTHORITY_BEFORE_RESERVATION, ATOMIC_SHARED_RESERVATION when shared budgets occur, safe state NO_NEW_DISPATCH and response REFUSE. It records requirements for the runtime; the evaluator alone does not perform native enforcement. Escalation is DECLARED_NONE or a complete destination-role, authority-reference and decision-required record. A required escalation creates no implicit message or additional permission. Its runtime delivery remains unqualified unless a runtime profile supplies that transition. Nonempty limitations yield UNSUPPORTED_RELATION, preserving the source obligation rather than discarding it.

## AUTH-002. Gate and qualification policy

Gate records materiality LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH, reversibility HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW/NONE, kind NONE/VERIFY/DECIDE, role and named criteria. HIGH materiality or LOW/NONE reversibility requires DECIDE. Otherwise MEDIUM in either dimension requires at least VERIFY. Only LOW materiality with HIGH reversibility permits NONE. A stricter gate is valid. A non-NONE gate requires a role and at least one named criterion; NONE requires null role and no criteria. Gate criteria use the same closed field predicates and each applies to the exact selected operation. Unsupported imported criteria are limitations.

VERIFY is evidence under an existing grant. It cannot enlarge the scope, suppress a limit or supply a grant. DECIDE requires an actual per-instance decision and organizational attestation of that decision. Both retain the reusable grant and its separate attestation. Qualification by exact field predicates does not invent approval of every instance when the envelope declares NONE.

The grant is valid only within temporal_validity's explicit half-open interval: from inclusive, until exclusive; a null until explicitly declares no fixed end. A required revocation declaration supplies provider, source and nonnegative exact max_age_seconds. Root, capacities and credentials have their own independent revocation declarations. No interval substitutes for withdrawal evidence. All authority instants use whole-second UTC under WCS2-003. The clock source equals the envelope's declared clock_source. An unavailable clock cannot establish readiness.

## AUTH-003. Independently selected root and complete records

Host selection is a separate host-owned input. Its complete root binds the source, work class, envelope, principal, permitted capacity ids, occupancy ids, providers and channels. It has its own validity interval, revocation declaration, explicit prior_acts_survive_expiry flag, separation rule and empty-or-unsupported limitations. The host establishes the real designator, actual selection, authenticity and nonrollback history outside authoring. The selection_evidence reference identifies retained host evidence; its existence does not prove those facts.

Each channel names its provider, validity interval and explicit prior_returns_survive_expiry flag. A return must have occurred within that channel's interval. Current reliance requires an active channel or its explicit survival flag. Removing a historical channel without a retained permitted reliance record cannot silently authenticate its old return. Providers and channels are selected independently of the envelope author.

An occupancy binds id, actor, role, principal, HUMAN or MACHINE kind, validity interval, provider and evidence reference. A capacity binds those identities and occupancy to the exact root and envelope digests, permitted act kinds, its own validity, revocation and current-reliance terms. CURRENT_ONLY requires current capacity validity. SURVIVE_EXPIRY permits reliance on an act made during capacity validity only within its separately declared reliance interval. Silence establishes neither survival nor self-attestation permission. Authority acts require HUMAN occupancy; machines may execute. Synthetic research designations and proxies remain explicitly unsupported in this first direct-record profile. Synthetic test fixtures assert fictional host facts for apparatus tests and cannot be represented as real authority.

A credential binds its actor and occupancy to one step/operation/interface, credential kind, validity, provider, evidence and revocation declaration. Every required credential kind comes from the work class. An actor's credential cannot replace their occupancy, the grant, its attestation or a gate decision. Exactly one selected credential supplies each required kind; missing and conflicting selections fail rather than choosing first or last.

The host_selection.authenticated_records array contains exact `{kind,record_digest,provider,channel}` tuples for every supplied occupancy, capacity, credential and return, with null channel for non-return records. Each tuple binds the complete supplied record, and its provider/channel must occur in the selected root. No extra tuple is admitted. The root's complete record is separately selected through host_selection, avoiding a recursive claim that the root authenticates itself. Conformance tests these equalities, not authentication or organizational truth.

## AUTH-004. Acts, returns and historical authority

GRANT names the envelope digest and expected grant id, grantor role and responsible principal. ORGANISATIONAL_ATTESTATION names the canonical digest of one actual GRANT or INSTANCE_DECISION and its attested_actor. It uses the organizational-attester role and that same principal. VERIFY and INSTANCE_DECISION name the exact operation digest, use the gate role and name exactly the declared criterion ids. Grant and attestation criteria are empty. An attestation cannot attest another attestation.

Each act records id, kind, actor, role, principal, occupancy id, capacity id, recorder, occurred_at, disposition, subject_digest, attested_actor and criteria. ACCEPT alone can support readiness. REJECT, PENDING and DEFER remain evidence. Exactly one grant and one grant attestation are selected, plus the gate's required act and, for DECIDE, its attestation. Extra or competing selected acts are ACT_CONFLICT. Historical superseded or rejected acts remain outside this selected set; the host must not conceal a material conflict by selection, and this profile supplies no conflict-resolution or latest-wins rule.

The act's actor, role, principal and occupancy match its complete capacity and occupancy. The capacity permits that act kind and exact envelope. Both records are permitted by the root. Each act occurs within root, capacity and occupancy validity and no later than evaluation. Its attestation occurs no earlier than its decision; equality is allowed. No grant-before-gate ordering is added. A source requiring additional ordering must express it in supported policy or remain unsupported.

Self-attestation is permitted only if the attestation capacity's may_self_attest and the independently selected root's allow_self_attestation are both true. The direct authority evaluator implements no other separation relation within one authority input. The lifecycle may separately require that one exact `VERIFY` or `INSTANCE_DECISION` actor differ from an actor retained for an exact completed predecessor under LIFE-007. General separation formulas remain unsupported. Grantor, principal, executor, attester and recorder are not collapsed into one identity.

Every act has exactly one return containing its full canonical Base64 act bytes, act digest, actor, provider, channel and evidence reference. Decode original bytes under WCS2-001; the complete decoded act must equal the selected act. The return's record digest must be present in the independently supplied authenticated-record tuple. A bare act digest or successful tool submission is insufficient. This establishes an attributed supplied record under a declared host premise, not a cryptographic signature by the person.

## AUTH-005. Revocation observations and required checks

For every selected act derive root and capacity revocation checks at its occurrence and at current evaluation. Deduplicate identical query subjects/times. Also derive current envelope and required-credential revocation checks. A query is exactly `{purpose:"REVOCATION",subject_digest,at,provider,source}`; its digest kind is `authority-query`. Each observation carries this exact query and digest, status, as_of, revision, revoked value and evidence reference. AVAILABLE requires complete values; other statuses require null as_of/revision/revoked. Its provider must be permitted by the selected root. AVAILABLE as_of is no later than query.at and query.at minus as_of is at most the corresponding declaration's max_age_seconds. An AVAILABLE as_of later than query.at is a phase-1 date-validity failure, INPUT_INVALID; freshness is measured only for an as_of at or before query.at. This is an explicitly authored observation-freshness allowance, not an automatic guarantee that authority remains unchanged before dispatch.

The observation inventory contains exactly one row per derived query. Exact repeated rows collapse; distinct rows for one query produce AUTHORITY_NOT_ESTABLISHED with OBSERVATION_CONFLICT. A missing row is EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE. An extra query or wrong query digest is DEPENDENCY_MISMATCH. UNAVAILABLE, INCOMPLETE, CONFLICT, stale or revoked observations cannot establish permission. Historical checks require their own exact historical query bindings; current observations cannot be relabelled as past evidence. Root/capacity expiry and withdrawal are separate. Root expiry permits current reliance on earlier acts only with prior_acts_survive_expiry; it never permits a new act outside its interval.

Derive the complete check set, never accept caller-authored SATISFIED rows. Checks cover exact work-class/proposal support, source/root bindings, scope substitution, materiality/gate floor, each per-action limit and condition, each selected act's occurrence-time capacity and occupancy, current reliance, root/capacity revocation at each required instant, current executor occupancy, every credential and its revocation, envelope validity/revocation, every gate criterion, return attribution and separation. Gate field criteria use the unchanged exact proposal at the gate's occurrence; independent time-varying facts need a later supported observation predicate contract. Shared-budget anchors are reported separately as unfulfilled requirements, never inserted as satisfied checks.

The result's checks use exact `{purpose,subject_digest,requirement_ref,at}` records sorted by their canonical bytes and deduplicated only when all fields are identical. Purpose tokens and construction are fixed in the schema companion inventory. The result binds the complete evidence bundle by `digest("authority-evidence",{host_selection,occupancies,capacities,credentials,acts,returns,observations,clock})`. No check claims to prove source English fidelity or the truth of host facts.

## AUTH-006. Refusal order and result

Apply these phases in order: (1) closed schema, canonical embedded bytes, scalar values, date validity and inventory ordering; (2) complete dependencies, identities and supported relations; (3) exact operation/field/executor/scope application and gate-floor checks; (4) selected act set, subjects, roles, dispositions, act-time records, returns and separation; (5) complete required observation inventory followed by current validity, freshness, revocation, conditions, limits and criteria; (6) construct readiness. A later policy outcome never hides earlier malformed input. Within a phase use the fixed code precedence in the schema companion; all refusals have empty path and a sorted reason list. The refusal includes no changed state because this evaluator is pure.

A successful result includes schema, specification_pin, status READY_FOR_RESERVATION, source/work-class/envelope/proposal/operation digests, resolved scope, actual_scope, selected act digests, complete satisfied check inventory, required_budgets and evidence_digest. The required_budgets list is the exact declared anchor set. Reservation must re-evaluate this complete input within its own atomic transition and satisfy those obligations against the same expected state revision. An old readiness receipt cannot be submitted as a replacement for the complete current authority input. Current revocation may prevent new admission; it does not make an already observed historical effect disappear.

## Compatibility boundary

This profile restates the source's reusable grants, eight-member envelope, exact one-step parameter substitution, strict gate floor, independent organizational attestation, current versus historical authority, complete attributable returns and independently selected root. Nominal type and predicate semantics are stated directly. Source conditions using unsupported calculus expressions, synthetic research designation, general delegation, quorum, separation relations beyond LIFE-007's exact cross-occurrence distinct-actor rule, conflicting-grant precedence and cross-step parameterized grants remain unsupported. No source requirement is silently replaced by a more restrictive per-instance approval or a weaker credential check.

The source binder's fully applicable result requires its limit consumers to have passed. This profile's readiness result deliberately stops before shared-budget checks. Only the combined reservation transition can supply the corresponding complete pre-dispatch judgment. Neither evaluator alone may claim that compatibility. Source confirmation, correspondence, organizational authorization of the artifact, production authentication and actual native mediation remain separately reviewed obligations.
