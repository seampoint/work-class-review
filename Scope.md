# Work-Class Governance Specification scope

The Work-Class Governance Specification Working Group develops a portable contract for deterministic governance of bounded consequential work.

The scope includes the normative representation and behavior required to define and evaluate:

- work-class identity, types, operations, participants, objects and composition;
- organizational authority, review evidence and deployment authorization;
- constraints, aggregates, shared reservations and exposure;
- work-instance lifecycle, permits, dispatch, native outcomes, disputes and completion;
- canonical records, state transitions, refusal precedence and deterministic judgments;
- the adapter protocol, implementation roles, qualification reports and conformance requirements.

The scope includes normative schemas and other exact machine-readable expressions of those requirements.

The scope does not include methods, software or services for eliciting source policy; translating prose; authoring, compiling or optimizing work-class definitions; training or prompting models; implementing a production host; intercepting agent or tool calls; supplying identity, observations, clocks, storage or native connectors; integrating enterprise systems; certifying deployments; or delivering training, support or consulting, except to the extent that the normative specification expressly requires externally visible behavior from an implementation.

The scope is representation- and implementation-independent. A conforming implementation does not require Seampoint authoring, runtime, host or consulting technology.

Changes to this scope are not retroactive.
